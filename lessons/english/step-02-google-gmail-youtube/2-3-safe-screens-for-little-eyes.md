# 🧒 Safe Screens for Little Eyes
*Let children watch videos made for them, and keep grown-up videos away.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Video That Should Not Have Appeared"

### Meet Mrs. Nambiar

👵 Mrs. Nambiar is 61 years old and lives in Kochi. Her grandson Kabir is six.

Kabir loves cartoons on her phone.
One afternoon, while she is in the kitchen, the next video on regular YouTube is not for children.
It is loud and frightening, and Kabir comes running to her, wide-eyed.

Mrs. Nambiar is upset with herself. She does not want this to happen again.

Her son shows her the **YouTube Kids** app.

📱 Her phone can:
- Show videos **made for children** in the YouTube Kids app
- Keep the grown-up videos away from small eyes
- Let Kabir watch his cartoons without worry

The next day she installs **YouTube Kids**, and Kabir watches his cartoons.
Nothing frightening appears.
*“Now I can finish cooking in peace,”* she smiles.

---

## 🤔 What If an Older Child Uses Regular YouTube?

Mrs. Nambiar's granddaughter Mira is 11 and likes regular YouTube for her school projects.

On regular YouTube, her mother turns on **Restricted Mode.**
It helps **hide videos meant for adults.**

💡 No setting is perfect. Sit with children sometimes, and ask them to tell you if anything upsets them.

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: For Young Children
✅ Install the **YouTube Kids** app from the Play Store.
✅ Open it for your child.

### Step 2: For Older Children on Regular YouTube
📱 Open the **YouTube** app.
✅ Tap your profile picture.

### Step 3: Open Settings
📱 Open: **Settings → General**

### Step 4: Turn On Restricted Mode
✅ Turn **Restricted Mode** ON.
✅ This helps hide videos meant for adults.
