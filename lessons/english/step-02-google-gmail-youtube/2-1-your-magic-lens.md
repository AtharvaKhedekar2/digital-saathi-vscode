# 🔍 Your Magic Lens
*Point your camera at anything, and your phone can translate it, name it or copy it.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Board I Couldn't Read"

### Meet Mr. Reddy

👴 Mr. Reddy is 69 years old and lives in Hyderabad. He is visiting his daughter in Pune for a month.

He loves his morning walk, but in Pune every shop board and bus board is written in Marathi.
He does not read Marathi.

One morning he wants to find the dairy his daughter mentioned.
He stands in the lane, looking at boards he cannot read. He feels lost, and he does not like to keep asking people.

Then his granddaughter Sneha shows him the **camera icon** in the Google search bar. It is called **Google Lens.**

📱 His phone can:
- **Translate** the words on a board into his language
- **Search** to tell him what something is
- **Copy** the words so he can use them

He points the camera at the board and chooses **Translate.**
The words change into his own language: *“Sai Dairy and Sweets.”*

*“So the phone has eyes now,”* he laughs, and walks into the shop.

## 🤔 What If You Cannot Read a Medicine Strip, or Do Not Know a Plant?

Mrs. Bhatt, 64, is given a medicine strip with a name she cannot read.
She points the camera at it and chooses **Text**, to copy the words, or **Search**, to find out what it is.

In the garden, her neighbour's plant has pretty flowers, but nobody knows its name.
She points the camera and chooses **Search**.

💡 Lens helps you read and learn. For medicines, always follow your doctor's advice.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: Open the Google Search Bar
✅ Find the **Google search bar** on your home screen, or open the Google app.

### Step 2: Tap the Camera Icon
📱 Tap the **camera icon**. This is **Google Lens**.

### Step 3: Point at Something
✅ Point the camera at a sign, a medicine strip or a plant.

### Step 4: Choose What You Want
✅ **Translate**: change the words into your language
✅ **Search**: find out what it is
✅ **Text**: copy the words

## 🎮 Interactive Activity: "Mr. Reddy in the Lane"

**Scenario**
A shop board is written in a language Mr. Reddy cannot read.

**Question**
What can he do? *(Tick all that are correct.)*

☐ Tap the camera icon (Google Lens) in the Google search bar
☐ Point the camera at the board
☐ Choose Translate
☐ Take a photo and delete it
☐ Ask the phone to switch off

**Answer**
✅ Tap the camera icon (Google Lens) in the Google search bar
✅ Point the camera at the board
✅ Choose Translate
❌ Deleting a photo will not translate anything
❌ Switching off the phone will not help
