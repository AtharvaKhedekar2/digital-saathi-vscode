# 📧 Your Digital Address
*One Gmail account opens school forms, class links and much more.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Form from the School"

### Meet Mrs. Hegde

👵 Mrs. Hegde is 65 years old and lives in Mangaluru. Her grandson Pranav is in Class 7.

One day Pranav's school sends a message: *“Please fill this Google Form for the science competition.”*
Mrs. Hegde has no email address, so she cannot sign in.
She has to wait for Pranav's parents to come home, and the last date is near.

She feels left out. *“Everything seems to need an email address these days.”*

Her daughter helps her create a Gmail account.

📱 Her phone can now, with one account:
- Send and receive **emails**
- Let her fill in **Google Forms** sent by a school or office
- Let her join a **Google Classroom** with the class code a teacher gives

She opens the Gmail app and taps **Create account.**
That evening, she fills in the Google Form herself.

*“Now I have my own digital address,”* she says proudly.

## 🤔 What If a Class or Office Asks You to Join?

Mr. Sawant, 68, wants to join a free online computer class.
The teacher gives him a **class code.**

With his Gmail account he joins **Google Classroom** using that code.

💡 Never share your Gmail password. A teacher or office never needs it.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: Open Gmail
✅ Open the **Gmail** app.

### Step 2: Create Your Account
📱 Tap **Create account**.
✅ Follow the steps and choose a name for your email address.

### Step 3: Fill In a Google Form
✅ When a school or office sends a Google Form link, tap it and fill it in.

### Step 4: Join a Google Classroom
✅ Open **Google Classroom** and tap to join.
✅ Type the **class code** your teacher gives you.
