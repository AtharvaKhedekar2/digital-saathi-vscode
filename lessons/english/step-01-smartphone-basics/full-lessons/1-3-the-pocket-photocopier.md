# 📄 The Pocket Photocopier

**Card subtitle:** Turn any paper into a clear PDF and send it on WhatsApp in a minute.

*Website point: "Turn your camera into a document scanner"*

---

## SECTION 1 · STORY

### 🎭 Story: "The Sunday Insurance Paper"

👤 **Meet Shobha Kulkarni.** She is 64 and lives in Kolhapur. She was a school teacher and she loves keeping her papers neat. Her son Aniket works in Pune and calls every Sunday.

**The situation.** One Sunday, Aniket calls in a hurry: *"Aai, the insurance office needs a copy of Baba's policy paper today. Can you send it?"* Shobha has the paper in her cupboard. But the photocopy shop is two kilometres away, and it is **closed on Sunday**.

**The problem.** She is worried. She tries clicking a photo of the paper with her phone, but the picture comes out dark, with a crooked edge and her own shadow across it. Aniket says he cannot read it.

**How the phone helps.** Her neighbour's daughter, Isha, shows her a better way. *"Aunty, your phone can work like a photocopy machine."* She opens the **Google Drive** app, taps **+**, then taps **Scan**. She points the camera at the paper. The phone finds the edges by itself and makes the picture clean and straight. Shobha taps to save it as a **PDF**.

**The happy ending.** In under a minute, she sends the clear PDF to Aniket on **WhatsApp**. He sends it to the insurance office from Pune. *"Aai, you are better than a photocopy shop!"* Shobha laughs. She puts the paper back in the cupboard. She has saved a trip, time and money.

## SECTION 2 · WHAT IF?

### ❓ What if the paper might get lost or spoilt?

**Meet Abdul Rehman**, 70, from Nagpur. He has a doctor's report and several prescriptions. Last monsoon, rain water leaked into his cupboard and spoilt some of his old papers. He was very upset.

Now he **scans** his important papers with the phone and keeps the clear copies safe. When the hospital counter asks for a copy of his report, he does not need to search the cupboard or visit a shop. He sends the PDF from his phone.

**A small safety tip.** Papers like Aadhaar and bank details are private. Send them only to people you know and trust, and never to strangers or in big WhatsApp groups.

**The lesson:** The scanner saves you a trip today and protects your papers for tomorrow.

---

## SECTION 3 · HOW TO SET IT UP

### 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. The Google Drive app is free and usually already on your phone. If not, download it from the Play Store.)*

**Step 1.** Open the **Google Drive** app.

**Step 2.** Tap the **+** button, then tap **Scan**.

**Step 3.** Place your paper flat, in good light. Point the camera at it and tap the round button to take the scan. Move the corners if the edges are not exact.

**Step 4.** Tap **Save**. It becomes a clear **PDF** that you can send on **WhatsApp**.

📱 **iPhone:** Use **Notes → Scan Documents**.

💡 **Tip:** Put the paper on a plain table, like a bed sheet. Avoid your own shadow on the page.

**MCQ 1.** Which app can turn your phone camera into a document scanner?
- A. The Clock app

- A. Clock → Alarm
