## SECTION 1 · STORY

### 🎭 Story: "One Button Could Make All the Difference"

👤 **Meet Mr. Sharma.** He is 68 years old and lives alone while his son works in another city.

Every morning, he goes for a walk in the neighbourhood park.

One day, while returning home, he feels dizzy and nearly falls. He is scared, his hands are shaking, and he cannot think whom to call first.

Luckily, his Android phone has **Emergency SOS** set up. He presses the **power button 5 times, quickly.**

📱 His phone can *(depending on how it was set up)*:
- Call for help
- Share his location with the people he chose
- Alert the family members saved as his emergency contacts

Soon his son Rahul is on the phone, and a neighbour is on the way. Mr. Sharma sits on a bench, safe and calm.

---

## SECTION 2 · WHAT IF?

### 🤔 What If Mr. Sharma Couldn't Unlock His Phone?

Imagine his hands are too shaky, or he cannot remember his PIN.

A passer-by wants to help. They tap **Emergency** on the lock screen. They can see what Mr. Sharma chose to add:

- 👦 Son: Rahul Sharma, with his phone number
- 👧 Daughter: Priya Sharma, with her phone number
- ❤️ Blood group and other medical details

💡 Only what you add is shown. Choose what you are comfortable with people seeing.

---

## SECTION 3 · HOW TO SET IT UP

### 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone. Use the search box in Settings if you cannot find a name.)*

**Step 1: Add Emergency Contacts.**
📱 Open **Settings → Safety & emergency → Emergency contacts**. Add your son or daughter, husband or wife, a close relative, and a trusted neighbour or friend.

**Step 2: Add Medical Information.**
📱 Open **Settings → Safety & emergency → Medical information**. Add your blood group, allergies, medicines and health conditions, and a short emergency note.

**Step 3: Turn On Emergency SOS.**
📱 Open **Settings → Safety & emergency → Emergency SOS** and turn it ON. Choose what happens when it starts (the choices depend on your phone): emergency call, location sharing, alerts to your emergency contacts.

**Step 4: Know the Shortcut.**
🚨 In an emergency, press the **power button 5 times, quickly.**

📱 **iPhone:** Open the **Health** app → **Medical ID**.

⚠️ **Safety tip:** Do not press the button 5 times "just to see". It may really call someone. Practise only after you have told your family. If you press by mistake, cancel it quickly.

## SECTION 8 · QUIZ

### 📝 Quiz: Your Silent Guardian

**5 Multiple-Choice Questions**

**MCQ 1.** What do you do to start Emergency SOS on many Android phones that have it turned on?
- A. Press the volume button once
- B. Press the power button 5 times, quickly
- C. Shake the phone twice
- D. Open the camera app

✅ **Answer: B.** Pressing the power button 5 times quickly calls for help.

**MCQ 2.** Where do you usually find the Emergency SOS setting?
- A. The Camera app
- B. The Calculator app
- C. The Gallery
- D. Settings → Safety & emergency

✅ **Answer: D.** The name can differ on some phones, so use the search box in Settings.

**MCQ 3.** What should you add so that a helper can see who to call without unlocking your phone?
- A. Emergency contacts on the lock screen
- B. A new ringtone
- C. A new wallpaper
- D. More apps

✅ **Answer: A.**

**MCQ 4.** What is the best thing to add besides contacts?
- A. Your bank PIN
- B. Your WhatsApp password
- C. Your medical information, such as medicines or allergies
- D. Your OTP

✅ **Answer: C.** Never put a PIN, password or OTP where others can see it.

**MCQ 5.** On an iPhone, where can you add a Medical ID?
- A. The Clock app
- B. The Health app
- C. The Weather app
- D. The Photos app

✅ **Answer: B.** Health app → Medical ID.

**3 True / False Questions**

**T/F 1.** You must unlock the phone before an emergency contact can show on the lock screen.
✅ **False.** The contact shows on the lock screen, so a helper can see it without the password.

**T/F 2.** It is a good idea to press the power button 5 times "for fun" to test it.
✅ **False.** It may really call someone. Practise only after telling your family.

**T/F 3.** You should keep your emergency contacts up to date.
✅ **True.** If a number changes, change it in the phone the same day.

**1 Scenario-Based Question**

**Scenario.** Mohan, 70, is alone at home and feels a sharp pain in his chest. His hands are shaking, and he cannot remember where his daughter's name is in the contact list. His phone has Emergency SOS turned on. What should he do?
- A. Search for the Settings app to turn the feature on
- B. Wait and hope the pain goes away
- C. Write a message to his daughter
- D. Press the power button 5 times, quickly, to call for help

✅ **Answer: D.** That is exactly what Emergency SOS is for. The feature must be turned on *before* the emergency.
