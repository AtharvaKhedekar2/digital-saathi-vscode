# 🛡️ Your Silent Guardian
*One press for help, and your family's numbers ready on your lock screen.*

---

## 🚨 Story: "One Button Could Make All the Difference"

### Meet Mr. Sharma

👴 Mr. Sharma is 68 years old and lives alone while his son works in another city.

Every morning, he goes for a walk in the neighbourhood park.

One day, while returning home, he feels dizzy and nearly falls.
He is scared, his hands are shaking, and he cannot think whom to call first.

Luckily, his Android phone has **Emergency SOS** set up.

He presses the **power button 5 times, quickly.**

📱 His phone can *(depending on how it was set up)*:
- Call for help
- Share his location with the people he chose
- Alert the family members saved as his emergency contacts

Soon his son Rahul is on the phone, and a neighbour is on the way.
Mr. Sharma sits on a bench, safe and calm.

## 🤔 What If Mr. Sharma Couldn't Unlock His Phone?

Imagine his hands are too shaky, or he cannot remember his PIN.

A passer-by wants to help. They tap **Emergency** on the lock screen.
They can see what Mr. Sharma chose to add:

- 👦 Son: Rahul Sharma, with his phone number
- 👧 Daughter: Priya Sharma, with her phone number
- ❤️ Blood group and other medical details

💡 Only what you add is shown. Choose what you are comfortable with people seeing.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone. Use the search box in Settings if you cannot find a name.)*

### Step 1: Add Emergency Contacts
📱 Open: **Settings → Safety & emergency → Emergency contacts**

✅ Add:
- Son or daughter
- Husband or wife
- A close relative
- A trusted neighbour or friend

### Step 2: Add Medical Information
📱 Open: **Settings → Safety & emergency → Medical information**

✅ Add:
- Blood group
- Allergies
- Medicines and health conditions
- A short emergency note

### Step 3: Turn On Emergency SOS
📱 Open: **Settings → Safety & emergency → Emergency SOS**

✅ Turn **Emergency SOS** ON
✅ Choose what happens when it starts *(the choices depend on your phone)*:
- Emergency call
- Location sharing
- Alerts to your emergency contacts

### Step 4: Know the Shortcut
🚨 In an emergency, press the **power button 5 times, quickly.**

📱 **iPhone:** Open the **Health** app → **Medical ID**.

⚠️ Do not press it 5 times "just to see". It may really call someone. Practise only after telling your family.
