# 🙅 Keep It Private
*Never type your OTP, passwords, Aadhaar, bank details or family photos into an AI chat.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Helpful Chatbot"

### Meet Mr. Tiwari

👴 Mr. Tiwari is 66 years old and lives in Varanasi. He has recently started using an AI chat app, and he enjoys it.

It answers his questions about history and cricket politely.
One day, he gets a message from his bank about a failed payment and an OTP.

He thinks: *“The AI is so clever. Let me ask it to fix this.”*
He starts typing: *“I got this OTP 482…”*

His thumb stops. A line from his class comes to mind: **“Treat an AI chat like talking to a stranger.”**

📱 What he remembers:
- **Never type** your **OTP, passwords, Aadhaar, bank details or family photos** into an AI chat
- An AI chat is like a **stranger**
- Your bank **is the only one** to sort out bank problems

He deletes what he typed. He calls his **bank** instead, using the number on the back of his card.

*“I almost handed my OTP to a machine,”* he tells his wife.

## 🤔 What If the AI Asks for Your Details?

Mrs. Rawat, 63, is chatting with an AI app, and it asks for her Aadhaar number “to help her better.”

She does not give it.

A good question can be asked without private details, for example: *“How do I link my Aadhaar to my bank?”*

💡 If any chat asks for private details, **stop**, and do not type them.

---

## 🛠️ The Rule — Step by Step

### Step 1: Think Before You Type
✅ Ask yourself: *“Is this private?”*

### Step 2: Never Type These
✅ **OTP**
✅ **Passwords**
✅ **Aadhaar**
✅ **Bank details**
✅ **Family photos**

### Step 3: Ask Without Details
✅ Ask your question in general words, with no private details.

### Step 4: Treat It Like a Stranger
✅ An AI chat is like **talking to a stranger.**
