# 🍲 What's in the Kitchen?
*Tell AI what you have at home, and get simple recipes for a quick dinner.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Empty Fridge Evening"

### Meet Mrs. Dhawan

👵 Mrs. Dhawan is 58 years old and lives in Delhi. Her husband will be home soon, hungry.

She opens the kitchen: rice, dal, one onion and a tomato.
Nothing fancy, and the shops are far.

*“What can I make with this?”* she wonders, and she feels a little stressed.

Her daughter says on the phone: *“Mummy, ask the AI.”*

📱 With an AI app she can:
- Tell it **what she has**
- Ask for a **quick dinner idea**
- Get **simple recipes and steps**

She opens the app and says:
**“I have rice, dal, onion and tomato. Suggest a quick dinner.”**

The app suggests a simple tomato-onion dal with rice, with the steps one by one.

In half an hour, dinner is on the table.
*“Simple, and tasty,”* her husband says.

## 🤔 What If You Have Allergies or Special Needs?

Mrs. Menon, 64, cooks for a husband who must avoid certain foods.
She adds this to her question: *“No fried food, please.”*

The AI changes its suggestion to suit her.

💡 AI suggestions are ideas. Use your own judgement about food, especially for health needs.

## 🛠️ How To Use It

### Step 1: Look at What You Have
✅ Check the kitchen: rice, dal, vegetables.

### Step 2: Open an AI App
✅ Open the **Gemini** or **ChatGPT** app.

### Step 3: Tell It What You Have
✅ Type or speak: **“I have rice, dal, onion and tomato. Suggest a quick dinner.”**

### Step 4: Follow the Steps
✅ Read or listen to the **simple recipe and steps.**
✅ Add anything you need, like “no fried food.”
