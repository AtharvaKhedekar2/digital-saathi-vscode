# 🕵️ Real or Fake?
*AI can make fake photos, videos and voices. Check before you forward.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Shocking Video"

### Meet Mrs. Agarwal

👵 Mrs. Agarwal is 63 years old and lives in Kota. She is a member of many family WhatsApp groups.

One morning, a video arrives in a group: a famous person says something shocking.
Everyone is forwarding it, with “Share with everyone!”

Mrs. Agarwal's thumb is ready to forward.

Then she remembers a lesson: **“AI can make fake photos, videos and voices.”**

📱 She remembers:
- AI can make **fake photos, videos and voices**
- Look for **odd hands or faces**
- **Check trusted news websites**
- **Do not share if you are not sure**

She watches the video again, slowly.
The person's fingers look strange, and the lips do not match the voice.

She searches for the news on a trusted news website. There is nothing about it.

She does not forward it. She tells the group: *“I think this is fake.”*
Later, others thank her.

---

## 🤔 What If It Is a Voice Message From a “Relative”?

Mr. Pillai, 68, gets a voice message that sounds like his nephew.
It asks for money urgently.

AI can copy voices, too. He calls his nephew on **his own number**, and finds that it was not him.

💡 When in doubt, **do not share.** It is better to stay quiet than to spread a lie.

---

## 🛠️ How To Check Before You Forward

### Step 1: Pause
✅ Do not forward at once. Take a moment.

### Step 2: Look Closely
✅ Look for **odd hands or faces.**

### Step 3: Check a Trusted Source
✅ Check **trusted news websites.**

### Step 4: Not Sure? Do Not Share
✅ If you are **not sure**, **do not share.**
