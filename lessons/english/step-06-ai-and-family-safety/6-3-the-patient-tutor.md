# 📚 The Patient Tutor
*Use AI to explain and to test you, not to write your homework.*

---

## 📖 Story: "Fractions at Last"

### Meet Divya

🧒 Divya is 12 years old and lives in Mysuru. She finds fractions very confusing.

Her mother cannot help much, and her tuition teacher is busy.
Her friend says: *“Just ask AI to write the homework. Copy it, and you are done!”*

Divya is tempted. But her teacher always says: *“If you copy, you do not learn.”*

Her mother suggests a better way.

📱 Instead of asking AI to **write the homework**, she can ask it to:
- **Explain** a topic **step by step**
- **Test** her with questions

Divya types: **“Explain fractions with a simple example, then give me 3 questions to try.”**

The AI explains with a chocolate bar cut into pieces, then gives her three questions.
She tries them. She gets two right and one wrong, and asks the AI to explain the wrong one again.

*“It is like a patient tutor who never gets tired,”* she says.
At school the next day, she answers a fractions question in class.

---

## 🤔 What If the AI Gives a Wrong Answer?

Aman, 13, asks the AI a maths question and gets an answer that does not look right.

AI can make mistakes. He checks it against his textbook and asks his teacher.

💡 Use AI to **understand**, and always check with your textbook or teacher. Never copy answers without understanding them.

---

## 🛠️ How To Use AI as a Tutor

### Step 1: Pick a Topic You Find Hard
✅ For example, **fractions.**

### Step 2: Ask for an Explanation
✅ Ask it to explain **step by step**, with a simple example.

### Step 3: Ask It to Test You
✅ Ask for **3 questions** to try.

### Step 4: Check and Learn
✅ Try the questions yourself.
✅ Ask it to explain again what you got wrong.
