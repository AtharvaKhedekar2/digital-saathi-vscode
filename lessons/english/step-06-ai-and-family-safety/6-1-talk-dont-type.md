# 🗣️ Talk, Don't Type
*Speak to AI in Hindi or Marathi, and listen to the answer.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Question in Marathi"

### Meet Mrs. Gaikwad

👵 Mrs. Gaikwad is 69 years old and lives in Nanded. She reads and speaks Marathi and understands a little Hindi.

Her grandchildren talk about “asking the AI” everything: recipes, stories, school work.
She feels left out. *“I cannot type in English. That is only for the young.”*

One evening, she wonders how to remove a stain from a silk saree.
Her granddaughter Sakshi says: *“Aaji, just speak to it.”*

📱 With an AI app like **Gemini** or **ChatGPT**:
- She taps the **microphone**
- She **speaks in Marathi** or Hindi
- She asks it to **reply in the same language**
- She **listens** to the answer

She opens the app, taps the microphone and asks in Marathi about the stain.
She adds: *“Please reply in Marathi.”*

A clear answer is spoken back to her, in her own language.
*“It is like talking to a knowledgeable neighbour,”* she says with a smile.

## 🤔 What If You Cannot Read the Answer Easily?

Mr. Shinde, 71, has tired eyes and cannot read small letters.
He asks his question by voice and **listens** to the answer instead of reading it.

💡 AI can make mistakes. For health or money, check important answers with a doctor, a bank or a family member.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Menu names can differ a little in different apps.)*

### Step 1: Open an AI App
✅ Open the **Gemini** or **ChatGPT** app.

### Step 2: Tap the Microphone
📱 Tap the **microphone.**

### Step 3: Speak in Your Language
✅ Speak in **Hindi or Marathi.**

### Step 4: Ask for a Reply in the Same Language
✅ Ask it to **reply in the same language.**
✅ **Listen** to the answer.
