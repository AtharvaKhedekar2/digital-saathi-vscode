# ⌚ Safe Steps to School
*A GPS smartwatch helps you stay connected with your child, used with care and respect.*

---

## 📖 Story: "Aanya Walks to Tuition"

### Meet Mr. and Mrs. Yadav

👨‍👩‍👧 Mr. and Mrs. Yadav live in Noida with their daughter Aanya, who is 9.

Every evening, Aanya walks to her tuition class, a short way from home.
Her parents want her to be independent, but they worry.
A smartphone feels too much for a nine-year-old.

They choose a **GPS smartwatch made for children.**

📱 The watch can:
- Let Aanya **call her parents with one tap**
- Let her use an **SOS button** in an emergency
- Show her parents **where the watch is**, in the **watch maker's official app**

Before she wears it, they sit down with Aanya and explain what it does, and why.
She helps choose her SOS contacts: Mummy, Papa and Dadi.

That evening, Mrs. Yadav opens the app and sees the watch at the tuition class.
She feels calm. Aanya feels trusted.

*“Mummy, it is like you are walking with me,”* says Aanya.

---

## 🤔 What If the Watch Cannot Find the Location?

One day, the app shows Aanya at home for an hour, but she is already at tuition.
The location is not always exact, and it can be late. The battery may be low, or the signal weak.

Mrs. Yadav does not panic. She **calls Aanya's watch**, then the tuition teacher.
Everything is fine.

💡 A watch **never replaces an adult's care.** Keep talking to your child, and teach her to **go to a trusted adult** if she needs help.

---

## 🛠️ How To Use It Safely

### Step 1: Set It Up With the Official App
✅ Set up the watch with the **watch maker's official app.**
✅ Never install a tracking app sent to you as a link.

### Step 2: Add Only Trusted Contacts
✅ Add **only trusted family members** as contacts.

### Step 3: Keep the Location Private
✅ **Never share** the watch's location publicly.
✅ Explain to your child what the watch does, and why.

### Step 4: Charge It Every Night
✅ **Charge the watch every night.**
✅ Remember: it **never replaces** an adult's care.
