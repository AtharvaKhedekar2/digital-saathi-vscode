# 📧 Your First Email Address
*One account opens Gmail, Google Classroom and Google Forms.*

---

## 📖 Story: “The Form Nobody Could Open”

### Meet Mrs. Bhatt

👵 Mrs. Bhatt is 66 years old and lives in Vadodara. She is a retired nurse.

Her grandson Aarav sends her a message: “Ajji, my school wants you to fill a form. It needs a Gmail address.”

Mrs. Bhatt has never made one. “It must be very difficult,” she thinks, and she feels a little worried.

📱 Aarti, a Digital Saathi volunteer, shows her that her phone can:
- Open the Gmail app
- Make a new account in a few minutes
- Use that one account for Gmail, Google Classroom and Google Forms

Together they type her name, choose a username and a password, and add her phone number.
Mrs. Bhatt writes the password on a small paper and keeps it in her cupboard at home.
*“It was not difficult at all,”* she says, smiling. *“Now I have my very own address.”*

---

## 🤔 What If You Forget Your Password?

Some weeks later, Mrs. Bhatt cannot remember her password.

She taps **Forgot password?** and Google sends a code to the phone number she added. She types it on the screen, and she is back in.

💡 That is why a phone number matters. And never tell the code to anyone, not even a helper.

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

### Step 1: Open Gmail and tap Create account
📱 Open the **Gmail app**, or go to **gmail.com**.
✅ Tap **Create account**, then choose **For myself**.

### Step 2: Type your name and choose a username
✅ Type your first and last name.
✅ Choose a username. It becomes your address, like name@gmail.com.

### Step 3: Choose a strong password
✅ Use at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers.
✅ Type it again to confirm.
✅ Write it on paper and keep it at home.

### Step 4: Add your phone number and finish
✅ Add your phone number when asked. It helps you get back in.
✅ Google may send a code to check it. Type the code on the screen only.
✅ Read the terms and tap **I agree**.
