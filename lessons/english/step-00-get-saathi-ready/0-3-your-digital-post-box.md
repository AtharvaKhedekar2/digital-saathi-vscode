# 📬 Your Digital Post Box
*Inbox, Compose, Sent and Spam: four words that make Gmail easy.*

---

## 📖 Story: “Photos from Dubai”

### Meet Mrs. Fernandes

👵 Mrs. Fernandes is 62 years old and lives in Goa. Her daughter works in Dubai.

Her daughter calls: “Mummy, I sent you the baby’s photos by email. Please check.”

Mrs. Fernandes opens Gmail and finds her daughter’s email waiting in the **Inbox**. She opens it and smiles at the photos.

She taps **Compose** and writes back: “Thank you, beta. Lovely!”

📱 Gmail on her phone:
- Keeps the emails sent to you in the **Inbox**
- Lets you write a new email with **Compose**
- Keeps your sent emails in **Sent**

Later she sees an email in **Spam** that says: “You have won ₹25 lakh!” She does not open it.
*“Good,”* she says. *“Spam is where the tricks go.”*

---

## 🤔 What If a Strange Email Is in Your Inbox?

Sometimes a suspicious email reaches the Inbox too. It may say it is from a bank and ask you to tap a link.

Mrs. Fernandes does not tap it. She deletes it, or shows it to her daughter.

💡 Do not tap links or open attachments from people you do not know.

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

### Step 1: Read your Inbox
📱 Open Gmail. The **Inbox** shows the emails sent to you.
✅ Tap an email to read it.

### Step 2: Write with Compose
✅ Tap **Compose**.
✅ Type the address in **To**, a **Subject** and your message.
✅ Tap **Send**.

### Step 3: Look in Sent
✅ **Sent** shows the emails you have sent.

### Step 4: Leave Spam alone
✅ **Spam** holds suspicious emails.
✅ Do not open them or tap their links.
