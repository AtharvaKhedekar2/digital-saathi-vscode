## 📖 Story: “The Little Button”

### Meet Mr. Bose

👴 Mr. Bose is 73 years old and lives in Kolkata. His grandson teases him: “Dadu, you are a student again!”

Mr. Bose opens his class on his phone. At the top he sees **Classwork**.

Inside he finds a task: “I finished 0.1 · Your First Email Address”.

He has already read that lesson and tried the activity on his phone. Now he taps the task to open it.

📱 In his class, his phone shows:
- **Lessons** to read
- **Tasks** to do
- A button to tell his Saathi that he has finished

When he has finished, he taps **Mark as done**.
That evening his Saathi writes: *“Well done, Mr. Bose!”* He feels very proud.

---

## 🤔 What If You Tap It by Mistake?

Mr. Bose once taps the button before he has done the task. He is worried.

He tells his Saathi, who helps him do the task properly. Nobody is angry. Learning takes time.

💡 A lesson post is only for reading. A task has a button to tap when you finish.

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

### Step 1: Open your class
✅ Open your class and tap **Classwork** at the top.

### Step 2: Open a task
✅ Tap a topic, then tap the task to open it.
✅ Lessons are only for reading. Tasks are for doing.

### Step 3: Do the task
✅ Read it slowly and do what it says.
✅ Ask a family member to sit with you if you like.

### Step 4: Tap Mark as done
✅ Tap **Mark as done**. If you see **Turn in**, tap that.
✅ Your Saathi can now see that you finished.
