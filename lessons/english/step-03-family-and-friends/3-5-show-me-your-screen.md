# 🖥️ Show Me Your Screen
*Share your screen on a video call, so family can guide you step by step.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Setting I Could Not Find"

### Meet Mrs. Thakur

👵 Mrs. Thakur is 67 years old and lives in Nashik. Her son Aditya lives in Bengaluru.

Her phone shows a message about an update, and she does not know what to press.
Aditya tries to explain on the phone: *“Amma, go to Settings, then tap the third one…”*
Mrs. Thakur gets confused. *“Which Settings? Where?”*
Both get tired and a little cross.

Then Aditya says: *“Amma, let me see your screen.”*

📱 Her phone can:
- **Share her screen** during a WhatsApp video call
- Let Aditya **see exactly what she sees**
- Let him **guide her** step by step

She taps the **share-screen button** during the video call.
Aditya sees her phone and says: *“Amma, tap the gear icon on the left. Yes, that one.”*

In five minutes, it is done. *“Why did we not do this before?”* she laughs.

## 🤔 What If Your Bank or Payment App Is Open?

Mr. Pillai, 70, is helped by his grandson on a video call.
Before sharing his screen, he **closes his bank and payment apps.**

That way, his grandson sees only what he needs to, and no private money details.

💡 Share your screen only with people you know and trust. Never share it with someone who called you out of the blue.

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(WhatsApp on Android and iPhone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: Start a Video Call
✅ Start a **WhatsApp video call** with a family member you trust.

### Step 2: Close Private Apps
✅ **Close your bank and payment apps** before you share.

### Step 3: Share Your Screen
📱 During the video call, tap the **share-screen button**.

### Step 4: Follow the Guidance
✅ Your family member sees your screen and guides you.
✅ Stop sharing when you are done.
