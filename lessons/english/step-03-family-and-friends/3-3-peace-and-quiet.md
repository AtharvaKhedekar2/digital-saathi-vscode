# 🔕 Peace and Quiet
*Mute noisy groups, and stop strangers from adding you to new ones.*

---

## 📖 Story: "Three Hundred Messages by Morning"

### Meet Mrs. D'Souza

👵 Mrs. D'Souza is 66 years old and lives in Mangaluru. She loves her church friends' group and her family group.

But one morning she wakes up to 300 new messages.
Good-morning pictures, jokes, forwarded videos, one after another, from groups she hardly reads.
Her phone buzzes all night, and her sleep is disturbed.

And that is not all. Last week, a stranger added her to a group about “easy money.”
She does not even know the people there.

Her son shows her two settings.

📱 Her phone can:
- **Mute** a noisy group, so it stops buzzing
- Let **only her contacts** add her to groups
- Keep **strangers** out of her chats

She taps a group's name, chooses **Mute notifications → Always.**
Then she opens **WhatsApp Settings → Privacy → Groups** and chooses **“My contacts.”**

That night, she sleeps well. *“Now my phone is quiet and my chats are mine,”* she says.

---

## 🤔 What If a Stranger Adds You to a Strange Group?

Mr. Prabhu, 70, finds himself in a group for “fast profit” that he never joined.
He is worried it is a scam.

Because he chose **“My contacts,”** from then on only people saved in his phone can add him.

💡 If a group looks strange or asks for money, do not reply and do not click on links. You can leave the group.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(WhatsApp on Android and iPhone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: Mute a Noisy Group
📱 Open the group and tap the **group's name** at the top.

### Step 2: Choose Mute Notifications
✅ Tap **Mute notifications → Always**.

### Step 3: Open Privacy Settings
📱 Open: **WhatsApp Settings → Privacy → Groups**

### Step 4: Choose Who Can Add You
✅ Choose **“My contacts.”**
✅ Now strangers cannot add you to groups.
