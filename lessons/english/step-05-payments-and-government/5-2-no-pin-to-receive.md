# 🔴 No PIN to Receive
*Your UPI PIN is only for sending money. Nobody needs it to give you money.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Buyer for the Old Fridge"

### Meet Mr. Dutta

👴 Mr. Dutta is 67 years old and lives in Guwahati. He wants to sell his old fridge online.

A buyer replies quickly: *“I will pay ₹8,000. Scan this code and enter your UPI PIN to receive the payment.”*

Mr. Dutta is excited. He is about to scan.

Then he remembers a rule from his class: **“Your UPI PIN is only for sending money.”**

📱 What he remembers:
- To **receive** money, you **never need your PIN**
- If anyone asks you to **enter your PIN** or **scan a code** to “receive” money, it is a **scam**
- Your PIN is a **secret**

He does not scan. He writes back: *“I do not enter my PIN to receive money.”*

The buyer vanishes.

*“If it were real, the money would come without my PIN,”* he tells his wife.

## 🤔 What If the Buyer Insists and Pressures You?

Mrs. Nair, 64, is selling an old sewing machine.
A buyer says: *“Please hurry, I am travelling. Just enter your PIN to confirm.”*

Pressure and hurry are part of the trick.

She says no, and blocks the number.

💡 Receiving money is easy: you share your UPI ID or QR code. You **never** enter a PIN for it.

---

## 🛠️ The Rule — Step by Step

### Step 1: Remember What the PIN Is For
✅ Your **UPI PIN** is only for **sending** money.

### Step 2: Never Enter It to Receive
✅ Nobody needs your PIN to give you money.

### Step 3: Never Scan to “Receive”
✅ If someone asks you to scan a code to **receive** money, **do not.**

### Step 4: Say No and Tell Someone
✅ Say no, block the number, and tell your family.
