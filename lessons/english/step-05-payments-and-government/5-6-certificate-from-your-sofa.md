## 📖 Story: "November Without the Queue"

### Meet Mr. Bose

👴 Mr. Bose is 78 years old and lives in Kolkata. He receives a pension, and every year he must prove that he is alive.

Every November he goes to the bank.
He stands in a long line, his knees ache, and the walk back feels very long.
Last year, he returned home exhausted and ill.

*“Must I do this every year?”* he asks his daughter.

His daughter says: *“Baba, you can do it from home. Just a face scan.”*

📱 With the **Jeevan Pramaan** and **AadhaarFaceRD** apps:
- A pensioner submits the **life certificate** from home
- The phone camera **scans the face**
- **No bank visit is needed**

His daughter helps him install the apps. He holds the phone in front of his face, and the camera scans it.

A message says his life certificate has been submitted.
*“No queue, no walking, and I did it from my sofa,”* he says in wonder.
