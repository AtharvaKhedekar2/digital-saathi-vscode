# ⚡ Tea in One Tap
*Pay small amounts, like tea or vegetables, with one tap and no PIN.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Morning Tea Stall"

### Meet Mr. Rathod

👴 Mr. Rathod is 70 years old and lives in Ahmedabad. Every morning he buys tea at the corner stall, and on the way back, vegetables.

He pays by UPI each time.
Every payment means: open the app, scan, type the amount, enter the PIN.
His fingers are slow, and a line forms behind him at the tea stall.

*“It is only ₹10 for tea. Why so many steps?”* he mutters.

His son tells him about **UPI Lite.**

📱 UPI Lite:
- You **add a small balance** to it
- **Small payments**, like tea or vegetables, go through with **one tap**
- **No PIN** is needed for these small payments

Mr. Rathod turns on **UPI Lite** in his UPI app and adds a small balance.

The next morning, he taps once at the tea stall. The payment is done.
*“Faster than counting coins,”* he smiles.

---

## 🤔 What If You Run Out of the Balance?

Mrs. Parmar, 66, uses UPI Lite for her daily small buys.
One day, her small balance runs out.

She simply **adds a little more** to UPI Lite. For bigger payments, she pays the normal way, with her PIN.

💡 Keep only a **small balance** in UPI Lite, enough for small daily payments.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Menu names can differ a little in different UPI apps.)*

### Step 1: Open Your UPI App
✅ Open the UPI app you already use.

### Step 2: Turn On UPI Lite
📱 Find **UPI Lite** and turn it **on.**

### Step 3: Add a Small Balance
✅ Add a **small balance.**

### Step 4: Pay Small Amounts
✅ Small payments, like tea or vegetables, now go through with **one tap**, **without entering your PIN.**
