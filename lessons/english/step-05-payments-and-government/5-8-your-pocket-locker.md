# 📁 Your Pocket Locker
*Keep your Aadhaar, PAN and certificates safe in DigiLocker, accepted as valid.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Missing Papers"

### Meet Mrs. Bhosale

👵 Mrs. Bhosale is 64 years old and lives in Satara. She is applying for a new gas connection.

The office asks for her Aadhaar and PAN copies.
She searches the cupboard. The Aadhaar is there, but the PAN card has gone missing.
She turns the house upside down. Her son has to come home early to help her.

*“Why do I keep losing papers?”* she says wearily.

Her son shows her **DigiLocker.**

📱 With **DigiLocker:**
- Official copies of your **Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence and certificates** are kept there
- They are **accepted as valid**
- You sign in with your **Aadhaar-linked mobile number**
- Use the **app** or **digilocker.gov.in**

She signs in with her Aadhaar-linked mobile number.
Her Aadhaar and PAN appear on her phone.

At the office, she shows the official copy from her phone.
*“Now my papers are always with me,”* she says.

## 🤔 What If You Lose Your Phone or the Paper?

Mr. Jadhav, 70, loses his driving licence.
Because he saved it in **DigiLocker**, he still has the official copy.

If he loses his phone, he can sign in again on another phone with his **Aadhaar-linked mobile number.**

💡 Sign in only on the **official** DigiLocker app or **digilocker.gov.in.** Never share your OTP with anyone.

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Ask a family member to help you the first time.)*

### Step 1: Open DigiLocker
✅ Open the **DigiLocker** app, or go to **digilocker.gov.in.**

### Step 2: Sign In
✅ Sign in with your **Aadhaar-linked mobile number.**

### Step 3: Find Your Documents
✅ Look for your **Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence** and certificates.

### Step 4: Show When Needed
✅ Show the **official copy** from your phone.
✅ It is **accepted as valid.**
