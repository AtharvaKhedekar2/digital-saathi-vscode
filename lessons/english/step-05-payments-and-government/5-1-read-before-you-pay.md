# 💳 Read Before You Pay
*After scanning a QR code, check the name and the amount before you enter your PIN.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Name on the Screen"

### Meet Mrs. Lal

👵 Mrs. Lal is 63 years old and lives in Chandigarh. She buys vegetables from the same vendor every morning.

One morning, she scans the vendor's QR code with her UPI app.
She is chatting and about to type her PIN, as she always does.

Then she remembers a tip: **“After scanning, read the name on the screen.”**

📱 What she checks:
- The **name** that appears on the screen: is it the shop or person she expects?
- The **amount**: is it right?
- Only then does she **enter her PIN**

She reads the screen. The vendor's name is *Ram Kumar.* But the screen shows a different name.

She **cancels** and asks the vendor.
He smiles: *“Madam, someone has stuck a different QR code over mine!”*

She pays the right QR, safely. *“A few seconds of reading saved my money,”* she says.

## 🤔 What If the Amount Is Wrong?

Mr. Bajaj, 68, scans a QR code at a medical shop.
The shopkeeper said ₹250, but the screen shows ₹2,500.

He **checks the amount** before entering his PIN, and cancels.
A small slip, caught in time.

💡 If the name or the amount does not match, **cancel**, and ask the shopkeeper.

---

## 🛠️ How To Pay Safely

### Step 1: Scan the QR Code
✅ Scan the shop's QR code with your UPI app.

### Step 2: Read the Name
✅ Read the **name** that appears on the screen.
✅ Is it the shop or person you expect?

### Step 3: Check the Amount
✅ Check that the **amount** is right.

### Step 4: Enter Your PIN, or Cancel
✅ If everything matches, enter your PIN.
✅ If not, **cancel.**
