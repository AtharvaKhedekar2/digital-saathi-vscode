# 🎭 The Secret Family Word
*Scammers can copy a voice. A secret code word keeps your family safe.*

---

## 📖 Story: "Dadaji, I Am in Trouble"

### Meet Mr. Qureshi

👴 Mr. Qureshi is 73 years old and lives in Bhopal. His grandson Zayan studies in another city.

One evening, his phone rings.
A voice that sounds exactly like Zayan says, in a panic: *“Dadaji, I am in trouble. I need ₹20,000 right now. Please do not tell Papa.”*

Mr. Qureshi's heart races. It is Zayan's voice. He is about to send the money.

But then he remembers: last Eid, the whole family agreed on a **secret code word.**

📱 What he knows:
- Scammers can **copy a voice using AI**
- A **code word** that only the family knows proves who is calling
- If the person cannot say it, **hang up**

He asks: *“Zayan, what is our family word?”*
The voice hesitates. Then the call cuts.

Mr. Qureshi hangs up and calls Zayan on **the number he knows.**
Zayan answers, fine and surprised. *“Dadaji, I never called you!”*

## 🤔 What If the “Relative” Is Crying and Rushing You?

Mrs. Dsouza, 66, gets a call from a “nephew” who is crying and says he has had an accident.
He asks her to send money immediately.

Crying and rushing are part of the trick. Scammers want you **not to think.**

She says: *“I will call you back.”* She hangs up and calls her nephew on his **own number.** He is safe.

💡 If a “relative” calls asking for money urgently, ask for the **family word**, or **hang up and call them back on the number you know.**

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

### Step 1: Choose a Word Together
✅ Sit with your family and agree on a **secret code word.**
✅ Pick something easy for you but hard to guess.

### Step 2: Say It, Do Not Post It
✅ Tell it in person or on a call.
✅ **Never write it** in a group, a message or a post.

### Step 3: Use It
✅ If a “relative” asks for money urgently, ask for **the word.**

### Step 4: Or Call Back
✅ If they cannot say it, **hang up** and call them on the **number you know.**
