# 📲 Only From the Store
*Install apps only from the Play Store or App Store, never from a link.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Free Subsidy App"

### Meet Mrs. Fernandes

👵 Mrs. Fernandes is 64 years old and lives in Margao, Goa. She is careful with money.

One morning, a message arrives on WhatsApp from an unknown number:
*“Get your government electricity subsidy now! Download our app. Tap the file below.”*

Below it is a file ending in **.apk.**

She loves the idea of saving money. Her thumb hovers over the file.

Then she remembers her lesson: **“An .apk file sent on WhatsApp or SMS can take over your phone and your bank account.”**

📱 She remembers:
- Install apps **only from the Play Store or App Store**
- A file ending in **.apk**, sent on WhatsApp or SMS, is **dangerous**
- **Never open one**

She does not open it. She deletes the message.

Later, she asks her son to check the electricity board's real app in the **Play Store.**
*“If it is real, it will be there,”* she says.

## 🤔 What If a Friend Sends the File?

Mr. Pinto, 69, receives an .apk file from a friend's number.
He thinks, *“My friend would not send anything bad.”*

But the friend's phone may have been taken over. A message from a known number can still be a trap.

He does not open it. He calls his friend, who is surprised.

💡 Even if it comes from someone you know, **do not open an .apk file.**

## 🛠️ How To Install Apps Safely

### Step 1: Open the Store
📱 Open the **Play Store** (Android) or the **App Store** (iPhone).

### Step 2: Search for the App
✅ Type the name of the app.
✅ Choose the one made by the **real company.**

### Step 3: Install
✅ Tap **Install.**

### Step 4: Never Open Files From Links
✅ **Never** open an **.apk** file sent on WhatsApp or SMS.
✅ If in doubt, ask a family member.
