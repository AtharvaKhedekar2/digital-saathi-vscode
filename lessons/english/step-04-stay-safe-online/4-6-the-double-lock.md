# 🔐 The Double Lock
*Two-step verification gives your WhatsApp and Gmail a second lock.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Code I Never Asked For"

### Meet Mrs. Awasthi

👵 Mrs. Awasthi is 66 years old and lives in Kanpur. She loves chatting with her sisters on WhatsApp.

One day, a stranger calls her: *“Madam, I am sending you a code. Please tell it to me, it is for your safety.”*
A code does arrive on her phone.

She nearly reads it out. But her niece Ritu had warned her: *“Aunty, a code is like the key of your house. Never give it to anyone.”*

She does not share it. The caller hangs up.

That evening, Ritu sits with her and adds a **second lock** to her WhatsApp and Gmail.

📱 The second lock:
- **WhatsApp:** a **6-digit PIN** that only she knows
- **Gmail:** **2-Step Verification**
- Even if someone learns her password or her code, they still **cannot get in without the second lock**

Mrs. Awasthi sets her own PIN, and writes it on a paper that goes in her cupboard at home.

*“Now my chats have a double lock,”* she says.

## 🤔 What If Someone Asks for Your PIN or Code?

Mr. Gupta, 70, gets a message: *“Please send the 6-digit PIN from WhatsApp to continue.”*

No genuine person or company will ask for it.

He does not reply. He tells his son.

💡 Never share your **PIN** or any **code** that arrives on your phone, with **anyone**, even if they say they are from a company or a bank.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: WhatsApp Two-Step Verification
📱 Open: **WhatsApp → Settings → Account → Two-step verification**

### Step 2: Turn It On
✅ Tap **Turn on.**
✅ Set a **6-digit PIN** that you will remember.

### Step 3: Gmail 2-Step Verification
📱 Open: **Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification**
✅ Follow the steps to turn it on.

### Step 4: Keep Your PIN Secret
✅ Write your PIN on paper and keep it safe at home.
✅ **Never** tell it to anyone.
