# 📢 The Golden Hour
*Lost money to a fraud? Call 1930 in the first hour, and call your bank.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Loan That Was a Trap"

### Meet Mr. Saxena

👴 Mr. Saxena is 71 years old and lives in Gwalior. He is a retired clerk.

He needed a small loan for a family function, and a caller offered one on easy terms.
He paid a “processing fee” through UPI. Then another fee. Then another.

Suddenly, he realises: no loan is coming. He has been cheated.
His stomach sinks. He feels ashamed and wants to hide it from everyone.

But then he remembers: **“The first hour after a fraud matters most.”**

📱 What he does:
- Calls **1930**, the **National Cyber Crime Helpline**
- Or reports at **cybercrime.gov.in**
- Calls his **bank** to **block his card or UPI**

He calls 1930 and tells them everything.
Then he calls his bank and asks to block his card and UPI.

Acting fast gives the best chance of stopping the loss.
His son sits beside him: *“Baba, you did not delay. That is what matters.”*

## 🤔 What If You Feel Too Ashamed to Tell Anyone?

Mrs. Trivedi, 67, loses money to a fake prize call, and she is too embarrassed to tell her family.
She waits two days.

Every hour of waiting makes it harder to act. Cheats are clever, and anyone can be fooled.

When she finally tells her daughter, they call **1930** together.

💡 There is no shame in being cheated. **Tell someone and act at once.**

### Step 2: Call 1930

### Step 3: Call Your Bank
✅ Ask your bank to **block your card or UPI.**

✅ Tell someone you trust, so they can help you.
