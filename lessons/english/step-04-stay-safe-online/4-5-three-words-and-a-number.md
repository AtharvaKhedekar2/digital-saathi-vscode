# 🔑 Three Words and a Number
*A long password you can actually remember keeps your accounts safe.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Password Was 1234"

### Meet Mr. Sinha

👴 Mr. Sinha is 68 years old and lives in Patna. He has a Gmail, a WhatsApp and a bank app.

For everything, he uses the same password: **1234.**
*“It is easy to remember,”* he says.

One day, his grandson Aman, who studies computers, hears this and is shocked.
*“Dadu, that is the first password a stranger would try!”*

Mr. Sinha is worried now. *“But I cannot remember hard passwords like @#X9q!”*

Aman smiles. He shows him a better way.

📱 The idea:
- Join **three or four unrelated words** with a **number**
- For example: **“Mango-River-Lamp-47”**
- It is **long**, **strong** and **easy to remember**
- Use a **different password** for every account

Mr. Sinha makes his own: **“Tulsi-Chai-Cricket-91.”**
He can picture a cup of tea and a cricket match, and he remembers it easily.

*“I made a strong password, and I did not even have to learn it by heart,”* he says proudly.

---

## 🤔 What If You Use the Same Password Everywhere?

Mrs. Khanna, 63, uses one password for everything.
If a thief learns it from one website, they can open her other accounts too.

That is why every account needs a **different** password.

💡 Write your passwords on paper and keep it in a **safe place at home**, not in your wallet or on your phone's wallpaper.

---

## 🛠️ How To Make a Strong Password

### Step 1: Pick Three or Four Words
✅ Choose words that have nothing to do with each other, like **Tulsi, Chai, Cricket.**

### Step 2: Add a Number
✅ Add a number that is not your birthday, like **91.**

### Step 3: Join Them
✅ **Tulsi-Chai-Cricket-91**

### Step 4: Make a New One for Each Account
✅ Use a **different password** for every account.
✅ Never share it with anyone who asks.
