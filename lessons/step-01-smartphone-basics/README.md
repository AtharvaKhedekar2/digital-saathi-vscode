# Step 01 · Smartphone Basics: story-based lessons

Six paste-ready lessons, one for each learning point of Step 01 on the Digital Saathi website. Every lesson starts with
a real-life story, then teaches the skill as a life skill, not a technology lesson. The facts (menu names, button
presses, example sentences) are exactly the ones in the website's approved Step 01.

| # | File | Card title | Website point | Badge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `1-1-your-silent-guardian.md` | 🛡️ Your Silent Guardian | Send an SOS in one press and show emergency contacts on your lock screen | 🏆 Safety Champion |
| 2 | `1-2-read-with-confidence.md` | 👓 Read With Confidence | Make text bigger and let your phone read it aloud | 🏆 Smart Reader |
| 3 | `1-3-the-pocket-photocopier.md` | 📄 The Pocket Photocopier | Turn your camera into a document scanner | 🏆 Paper-to-PDF Pro |

| 5 | `1-5-your-pocket-assistant.md` | 🗣️ Your Pocket Assistant | Set medicine and bill reminders just by speaking | 🏆 Reminder Master |

**Suggested Step 01 completion badge:** 🏆 **Mobile Master**, for a learner who has earned all six badges. (Not part of
the six lessons; add it if you like the idea.)

## Two versions of every lesson

| Folder | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `story-pages/` | **Short story-page version** (about 600 words each): story, what-if, how to set it up, activity, benefits, reflection. Short lines, emoji headings, "Open: Settings → …" and "Add:" lists. | The **Story Page** that learners read on the website, in Classroom or in an app |
| (this folder) | **Full lesson** (about 1,800 words each): everything above plus the infographic prompt, a 9-question quiz, the badge and the learning objectives | The infographic, the quiz and the badge; also the full reference |

The facts in both versions are the same. In the story pages, every activity has one or two wrong options, so ticking
everything is not an easy win.

## What is in every lesson (the same 10 sections)

Card title and subtitle · 1 Story · 2 What if? · 3 How to set it up (4 steps) · 4 Interactive activity (scenario, question,
tick boxes, correct answer, badge) · 5 Real-life benefits (4) · 6 Reflection question · 7 Infographic design prompt ·
8 Quiz (5 multiple choice, 3 true/false, 1 scenario question, all with answers) · 9 Badge name · 10 Learning objectives (3).

## Where each part goes (the six displays)

| Display | Use these sections | How |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Learning card** | Card title + subtitle (top of the file) | The tile learners tap. Add the card's emoji and the badge name. |
| **2. Story page** | 1 Story, 2 What if?, 3 How to set it up, 5 Benefits, 6 Reflection | One scrolling page, in this order. |
| **3. Infographic** | 7 Infographic design prompt | Paste the quoted prompt into an image tool, then add the exact text yourself (see the note below). |
| **4. Interactive activity** | 4 Interactive activity | A checkbox question. Give the "correct answer" text as feedback. Awarded badge = the lesson's badge. |
| **5. Quiz** | 8 Quiz | 9 questions: 5 multiple choice, 3 true/false, 1 scenario. Every answer has a one-line reason, which works well as feedback. |
| **6. Completion badge** | 9 Badge name + 10 Learning objectives | Show after the quiz. Objectives double as a "you can now…" summary. |

**Google Classroom suggestion:** one *Topic* per lesson. Inside it, post (1) the story page as a *Material*, (2) the
infographic as a *Material*, (3) the activity as an ungraded *Assignment*, (4) the quiz as a *Quiz assignment* (Google
Forms) with the answers in the files, and (5) the badge as a closing *Material*. Do not attach outside links.

## Things to know

- **Characters are fictional.** The names (Kamla Devi, Ramesh Iyer, …) are invented for the stories and are not real people.
- **Phones differ.** Menu names change between brands. Every "How to set it up" says so and tells learners to use the search
  box in Settings. The SOS lesson also warns not to press the power button five times "for fun".
- **Quiz answers.** Right answers are marked ✅, and their positions (A–D) are mixed across the questions. If you build the
  quiz in Google Forms, you can also switch on shuffling.
- **Infographic prompts.** They use the Digital Saathi colours (green #2C5841, mustard #F2C14E, cream #F8F6F1). AI image
  tools often garble words, so generate the pictures first, then add the exact headings and steps in Canva (or similar).
- **No statistics and no outside links** are used anywhere.
- **Not connected to the website or the Classroom script yet.** These are separate documents. Hindi and Marathi versions
  have not been written yet.
