# 🗣️ Your Pocket Assistant
*Just say it, and your phone will remember your medicines and bills.*

---

## 📖 Story: "I Told My Phone, and It Remembered"

### Meet Mr. Nair

👴 Mr. Nair is 74 years old and lives in Kochi. He retired from the railways.

Every morning he has a small routine: tea, newspaper, and a tablet for his blood pressure at 9 am.

This month, his granddaughter's wedding fills the house with guests.
His routine has become muddled.

Twice this week he forgot his 9 am tablet.
Then a letter came: his **electricity bill** was late, and he had to pay a **late fee.**

*"My memory is not what it used to be,"* he says sadly.

That evening, his son Anil says: *"Appa, you do not need to remember everything. Just tell your phone."*

📱 His phone can:
- Set a reminder when you **speak**, with no typing
- Remind you at the **right time**
- Remember **medicines, bills** and more

Mr. Nair says: **"Hey Google, remind me to take my medicine every day at 9 am."**
The phone confirms.

Next morning at 9, the phone rings gently. He takes his tablet and taps **Done.**
On the 5th, it reminds him to pay the bill, with no late fee.

## 🤔 What If Typing Is Hard, or Your Hands Are Busy?

Mrs. Krishnan has stiff fingers from arthritis.
Today her hands are covered in flour while she makes chapatis.
She remembers she must book a blood test.

She does not wash her hands or look for her phone's keyboard.
She simply says: **"Hey Google, remind me to book my blood test tomorrow at 10 am."**

Done in seconds, with no typing.

💡 Reminders help you remember. They do not replace your doctor's advice. Take medicines exactly as your doctor told you.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. "Hey Google" must be turned on. If your phone does not answer, ask a family member to switch it on once.)*

### Step 1: Wake Up Your Phone
✅ Say clearly: **"Hey Google"**
✅ Wait for the phone to listen.

### Step 2: Set a Medicine Reminder
✅ Say: **"Remind me to take my medicine every day at 9 am."**

### Step 3: Set a Bill Reminder
✅ Say: **"Remind me to pay the electricity bill on the 5th."**

### Step 4: When It Rings
✅ Your phone reminds you on time.
✅ Tap **Done** when you have finished.

💡 Speak slowly in a quiet place. If the phone did not understand, say it again.
