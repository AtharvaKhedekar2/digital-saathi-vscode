## 📖 Story: "The Message I Could Finally Read"

### Meet Mr. Iyer

👴 Mr. Iyer is 71 years old and lives in Chennai. He used to be a bank clerk and he still loves reading.

Lately, his eyes get tired quickly. The letters on his phone look like tiny ants.

One evening, a message arrives from the gas agency about his cylinder delivery.
He holds the phone close, then far, then close again. He cannot read the time slot.
He misses the delivery, and he is too shy to ask anyone for help.

The next Sunday, his daughter Meena visits.
She makes the letters **bigger** and turns on **Select to Speak**.

📱 His phone can now:
- Show big, clear letters
- Read any text aloud when he taps it
- Help him even when he has no glasses

The next message arrives. Mr. Iyer reads it by himself, and the phone reads the time slot aloud.
*"My phone is finally speaking my language,"* he laughs.

## 🤔 What If You Forgot Your Glasses?

Mrs. Menon is at the vegetable market. Her glasses are at home.
A message arrives from the pharmacy about her husband's medicine.

She taps the message, and her phone **reads it aloud**.
She learns that the medicine is ready for pickup today.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone. Use the search box in Settings if you cannot find a name.)*

### Step 1: Make the Letters Bigger
📱 Open: **Settings → Display → Font size**

✅ Slide towards bigger letters until reading feels easy.

### Step 2: Turn On Select to Speak
📱 Open: **Settings → Accessibility → Select to Speak**

✅ Turn it ON.

### Step 3: Listen
✅ Tap any text on the screen.
✅ Your phone reads it aloud.

### Step 4: Keep It Private
✅ Use earphones for bank and health messages.
✅ Never share your OTP with anyone who offers to "read it for you".

📱 **iPhone:** Use **Spoken Content** (Settings → Accessibility).
