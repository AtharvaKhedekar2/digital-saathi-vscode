## 📖 Story: "Closed on Sunday, Solved in a Minute"

### Meet Mrs. Kulkarni

👩 Mrs. Kulkarni is 64 years old and lives in Kolhapur. She was a school teacher and she keeps her papers neat.

On Sunday, her son Aniket calls from Pune in a hurry.
*"Aai, the insurance office needs a copy of Baba's policy paper today."*

The paper is in her cupboard.
But the photocopy shop is far away, and it is **closed on Sunday.**

She tries a photo with her phone. It comes out dark and crooked, with her shadow on it.
Aniket cannot read it.

Then her neighbour's daughter, Isha, shows her the **scanner** hidden in her phone.

📱 Her phone can:
- Find the edges of the paper by itself
- Make the picture clean and straight
- Save it as a clear **PDF**
- Send it on **WhatsApp**

In under a minute, the PDF reaches Aniket.
*"Aai, you are better than a photocopy shop!"*

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. The Google Drive app is free and is usually already on your phone.)*

### Step 1: Open Google Drive
✅ Open the **Google Drive** app.

### Step 2: Start the Scanner
📱 Tap **+**, then tap **Scan**.

### Step 3: Scan the Paper
✅ Place the paper flat, in good light.
✅ Point the camera at it and tap the round button.
✅ Move the corners if the edges are not exact.

### Step 4: Save and Send
✅ Tap **Save**. It becomes a clear **PDF**.
✅ Share it on **WhatsApp**.

📱 **iPhone:** Use **Notes → Scan Documents**.
