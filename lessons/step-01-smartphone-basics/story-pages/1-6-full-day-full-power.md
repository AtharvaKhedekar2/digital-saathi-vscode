# 🔋 Full Day, Full Power
*Simple habits that keep your phone alive from morning till night.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Phone That Lasted Till the Video Call"

### Meet Mrs. Joshi

👵 Mrs. Joshi is 67 years old and lives in Nagpur.

Her granddaughter Ananya studies in another country.
They have a promise: a **video call every evening at 8 o'clock.**

But lately, Mrs. Joshi's phone goes dead around 5 or 6 in the evening.
Yesterday the call ended halfway because the battery ran out.
Ananya was sad, and Mrs. Joshi felt helpless.

*"This phone gets tired before I do!"*

Her neighbour, Mr. Kelkar, shows her four small habits.

📱 Her phone can last longer if she:
- Turns on **Battery Saver**
- Keeps the **screen brightness low**
- **Closes** the apps she is not using
- Switches off **Location and Bluetooth** when she does not need them

At 8 o'clock that evening, her phone still has enough charge for the whole video call.
*"My phone lasted the whole day, just like me!"*

## 🤔 What If You Cannot Charge Your Phone for a Long Time?

Mr. Singh is travelling by train for many hours.
There is no charging point near his seat, and his phone is getting low.
He still needs it to show his ticket and to call his daughter at the station.

He turns on **Battery Saver**, keeps the **brightness low**, closes unused apps,
and switches off **Location and Bluetooth.** He does not watch videos on the way.

His phone lasts until the station, and he calls his daughter in time.

💡 If your battery is very low, keep the last bit of charge for the most important thing: a call to your family.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone. Use the search box in Settings if you cannot find a name.)*

### Step 1: Turn On Battery Saver
📱 Open: **Settings → Battery**

✅ Turn **Battery Saver** ON.

### Step 2: Keep the Screen Dim
✅ Swipe down from the top of the screen.
✅ Move the brightness slider towards low.

### Step 3: Close Unused Apps
✅ Open **Recent apps**.
✅ Close the apps you are not using.

### Step 4: Switch Off What You Do Not Need
✅ Switch off **Location** and **Bluetooth** when you do not need them.
✅ Switch them on again when you do, for example Location when you use Maps.
