# 🔒 The Safe Handover
*Let the little ones watch their rhymes, without wandering into your messages.*

## 📖 Story: "Aarav Watches, Aaji's Phone Stays Safe"

### Meet Mrs. Deshmukh

👵 Mrs. Deshmukh is 62 years old and lives in Mumbai with her son's family.

Her grandson Aarav is five. Every evening he runs to her: *"Aaji, phone! Rhymes!"*

Last week, while watching his rhyme, he tapped a corner of the screen.
He landed in her WhatsApp and sent a string of smileys and a blank voice note to her friend.

Now she feels nervous every time she hands over the phone.
She sits right beside him and keeps taking it back.

Then her daughter-in-law, Pooja, shows her **App pinning.**

📱 Her phone can now:
- Stay on **one app** only
- Ask for her **PIN** before anyone can leave that app
- Keep her chats, photos and payment apps out of reach

Aarav taps everywhere, but the screen stays on his rhymes.
Mrs. Deshmukh sips her tea in peace.

## 🤔 What If You Cannot Watch the Child Every Second?

Mrs. Sheikh is cooking for a family wedding.
Her cousin's eight-year-old, Zoya, wants to play a game on her phone.

The phone has a **payment app**, photos and chats.
Before she hands it over, Mrs. Sheikh **pins the game.**

Zoya can play. She cannot open the payment app or the chats, even by accident,
because leaving the game needs Mrs. Sheikh's **PIN**.

💡 Never tell the child your PIN.
💡 When you take the phone back, enter your PIN and unpin the app.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone. Use the search box in Settings and type "pin" if you cannot find it.)*

### Step 1: Turn On App Pinning
📱 Open: **Settings → Security** (or search "pin")

✅ Turn **App pinning** ON.
✅ If it asks, choose to ask for your **PIN** before unpinning.

### Step 2: Open the App
✅ Open the app you want the child to use, for example the rhyme video.

### Step 3: Pin It
📱 Open **Recent apps** (use the button or swipe your phone uses for this).

✅ Tap the app's **icon** at the top.
✅ Choose **Pin**.

### Step 4: Hand Over the Phone
✅ The child cannot leave that app without your **PIN**.
✅ When you take the phone back, enter your PIN to unpin.

📱 **iPhone:** Use **Guided Access**.

**Answer**
✅ Turn on App pinning in Settings → Security
✅ Open the video app, go to Recent apps, tap its icon and choose Pin
✅ Make sure her PIN is needed to leave the app

❌ An open phone lets her tap into other apps
