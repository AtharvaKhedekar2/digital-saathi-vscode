# 🔋 Full Day, Full Power

**Card subtitle:** Simple habits that keep your phone alive from morning till night.

*Website point: "Make your battery last the whole day"*

---

## SECTION 1 · STORY

### 🎭 Story: "The Video Call at Eight"

👤 **Meet Parvati Joshi.** She is 67 and lives in Nagpur. Her granddaughter, Ananya, studies in another country. They have a special promise: a **video call every evening at 8 o'clock**, so that Ananya can show her new things, and Parvati can show her the garden and the new rangoli.

**The situation.** Every day, Parvati Joshi looks forward to the 8 o'clock call from the moment she wakes up.

**The problem.** But lately, her phone has been going dead around 5 or 6 in the evening. Yesterday, she plugged it in late, and the call ended halfway because the battery ran out. Ananya was sad. Parvati Joshi felt helpless: *"This phone gets tired before I do!"*

**How the phone helps.** Her neighbour, Mr. Kelkar, a retired engineer, drops in. He shows her four small habits. He opens **Settings → Battery** and turns on **Battery Saver**. He lowers the **screen brightness**. He shows her how to **close the apps she is not using**. And he switches off **Location** and **Bluetooth**, because she does not need them all day.

**The happy ending.** That evening, at 8 o'clock, Parvati Joshi's phone still has enough charge for the whole video call. She shows Ananya the rangoli, and they talk for a long time. *"My phone lasted the whole day, just like me,"* she says with a laugh.

## SECTION 2 · WHAT IF?

### ❓ What if you cannot charge your phone for a long time?

**Meet Harbhajan Singh**, 73, from Amritsar. He is travelling by train to his daughter's home, and the journey will take many hours. There is no charging point near his seat. His phone is already getting low, and he needs it to show his ticket and to call his daughter when he reaches the station.

He turns on **Battery Saver**, keeps the **brightness low**, closes the apps he is not using, and switches off **Location and Bluetooth** because he does not need them on the train. He does not play videos. His phone lasts until he reaches the station, and he is able to call his daughter in time.

**A small safety tip.** If your phone gets very low, keep a few minutes of battery for the most important thing: a call to your family.

**The lesson:** Small habits can give your phone more time when you need it most.

---

## SECTION 3 · HOW TO SET IT UP

### 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Your phone's menu names may be a little different. Use the search box in Settings if you cannot find a name.)*

**Step 1.** Open **Settings → Battery** and turn on **Battery Saver**.

**Step 2.** Keep the **screen brightness low**. (Swipe down from the top of the screen and move the brightness slider to the left.)

**Step 3.** **Close the apps you are not using.** (Open Recent apps and close the ones you do not need.)

**Step 4.** **Switch off Location and Bluetooth** when you do not need them. (Swipe down from the top and tap their icons.)

💡 **Tips:**
- Turn them on again when you need them, for example, Location when you use Maps.
- Do these small things every day, and the battery will thank you.

## SECTION 4 · INTERACTIVE ACTIVITY

### 🎮 Interactive Activity: "Jaya's Dying Phone"

**Scenario.** Jaya, 65, lives in Bhopal. Her phone is dead by 3 o'clock every afternoon, and she wants it to last until bedtime.

**Question.** Which habits will help Jaya's battery last longer? *(Tick all that are correct.)*

- ☐ Turn on **Battery Saver** in **Settings → Battery**
- ☐ Keep the **screen brightness low**
- ☐ **Close apps** she is not using
- ☐ Switch off **Location and Bluetooth** when she does not need them
- ☐ Keep the screen at full brightness all day
- ☐ Keep ten apps open all day, "just in case"

**✅ Correct answer:** The first four:
- Turn on Battery Saver in Settings → Battery
- Keep the screen brightness low
- Close apps she is not using
- Switch off Location and Bluetooth when she does not need them

*Why the others are wrong:* A very bright screen and many open apps use more battery.

## SECTION 8 · QUIZ

### 📝 Quiz: Full Day, Full Power

**5 Multiple-Choice Questions**

**MCQ 1.** Where do you turn on Battery Saver?
- A. The Camera app
- B. The Contacts app
- C. Settings → Battery
- D. The Clock app

✅ **Answer: C.**

**MCQ 2.** Which screen brightness helps the battery last longer?
- A. Full brightness all day
- B. Flashing brightness
- C. It makes no difference
- D. Low brightness

✅ **Answer: D.**

**MCQ 3.** What should you do with apps you are not using?
- A. Close them
- B. Open more of them
- C. Share them with friends
- D. Lock them with a PIN

✅ **Answer: A.**

**MCQ 4.** Which two things can you switch off when you do not need them?
- A. The screen and the speaker for ever
- B. Location and Bluetooth
- C. The phone's SIM card for ever
- D. The battery itself

✅ **Answer: B.**

**MCQ 5.** Why is it a good idea to save some battery for the end of the day?
- A. So the phone looks like new
- B. Because batteries like to rest at night
- C. So you can still make an important call
- D. It does not matter

✅ **Answer: C.**

**3 True / False Questions**

**T/F 1.** A very bright screen uses more battery than a dim screen.
✅ **True.**

**T/F 2.** Closing apps you are not using can help your battery last longer.
✅ **True.**

**T/F 3.** Location and Bluetooth must stay on all day for the phone to work.
✅ **False.** Switch them off when you do not need them, and on again when you do (for example, Location when you use Maps).

**1 Scenario-Based Question**

**Scenario.** Gopal, 72, is going to a wedding and needs his phone for photos and for a call to his son when he returns. His battery is low and there is no charger near him. What is the best thing to do?
- A. Turn on Battery Saver, keep the brightness low, close unused apps and switch off Location and Bluetooth
- B. Play videos all evening
- C. Keep ten apps open "just in case"
- D. Turn the brightness to the maximum

✅ **Answer: A.** Small habits save battery for the things that matter.
