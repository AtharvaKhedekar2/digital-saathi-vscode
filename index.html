    04Certificate

    Earn your certificate.

    1. 📚Finish all six learning modules.
    2. 🙌Do the hands-on practice on your own phone with a Saathi.
    3. 📝Score 14 out of 20 (70%) or more in the Post-Assessment Quiz — retakes are free.
    4. 📩Show your result to a Digital Saathi volunteer — after checking it, the team sends your certificate to you later.
    Start the post-assessment

    Certificates are free. We will never ask for money, OTP or bank details for a certificate.
