01About Digital Saathi

Saathi means companion.

Someone who walks beside you — patiently, in your language — until you feel confident on your own.

Made for

  • Senior citizens
  • First-time smartphone users
  • People looking for work
  • Homemakers & small shopkeepers

Many people around us can make a phone call, but feel nervous about UPI payments, online forms and scam messages.

Digital Saathi is a student-led community initiative that changes this, one patient lesson at a time.
