# 👪 A Guiding Hand
*Use Google Family Link to guide your child's phone: screen time, apps and location.*

---

## 📖 Story: "Arjun's First Phone"

### Meet Mrs. Pawar

👩 Mrs. Pawar is 40 years old and lives in Thane. Her son Arjun is 10, and he has just been given his first phone for online classes.

She is happy for him, but she is also anxious.
*“How long is he on it? What apps is he downloading? Where is the phone when he goes to tuition?”*

Constant checking makes both of them irritable.
Arjun says: *“Mummy, you keep taking my phone!”*

Then she learns about **Google Family Link.**

📱 With **Family Link**, from her own phone she can:
- Set **screen-time limits**
- **Approve the apps** he downloads
- **See where the phone is**

She installs **Google Family Link** on her phone and links Arjun's phone.
She sits down with Arjun and explains what each setting does, and why.

*“It is not to spy, Arjun. It is so that we both feel safe.”*
Arjun nods. He even asks for a little more screen time on Sundays, and she agrees.

---

## 🤔 What If a Child Wants to Download a New App?

Arjun wants a new game.
On his phone, the app waits for his mother's **approval**.

Mrs. Pawar looks at the app, asks him about it, and says yes.

💡 Talk to your child. Family Link works best when children understand why the rules exist, and when the rules grow with their age.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Ask a family member to help you the first time.)*

### Step 1: Install Family Link on Your Phone
✅ Install **Google Family Link** on **your** phone (the parent's).

### Step 2: Link Your Child's Phone
✅ Follow the steps to **link your child's phone.**

### Step 3: Set Limits and Approvals
✅ Set **screen-time limits.**
✅ Choose to **approve the apps** they download.

### Step 4: Check and Talk
✅ **See where the phone is.**
✅ Talk to your child about the rules, and why you have them.
