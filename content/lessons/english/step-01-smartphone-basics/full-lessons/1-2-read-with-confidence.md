## SECTION 1 · STORY

### 🎭 Story: "The Gas Delivery Message"

👤 **Meet Ramesh Iyer.** He is 71 and lives in Chennai. He used to be a bank clerk, and he loves reading the newspaper with his filter coffee. Lately, his eyes get tired quickly, and the words on his phone look like tiny ants.

**The situation.** One evening, a message arrives on his phone: it is from the gas agency about his cylinder delivery. Ramesh Iyer holds the phone close, then far, then close again. He cannot read the time slot. He is too proud to ask the delivery boy and too shy to ask his neighbour. He misses the delivery.

**The problem.** The phone is working perfectly. The letters are simply too small for his eyes, and he does not know that the phone can change that.

**How the phone helps.** The next Sunday, his daughter Meena visits. She opens **Settings → Display → Font size** and slides it to make the letters bigger. Then she opens **Settings → Accessibility** and turns on **Select to Speak**. *"Appa, tap any words on the screen, and the phone will read them to you."*

**The happy ending.** The next time a message arrives, Ramesh Iyer reads it comfortably, big and clear. He taps the words, and the phone reads the time slot aloud. He is delighted. Now he reads the news on his phone every morning, in letters as big as a newspaper headline. *"My phone is finally speaking my language,"* he laughs.

## SECTION 2 · WHAT IF?

### ❓ What if you forgot your glasses?

**Meet Lakshmi Menon**, 66, from Kochi. She is at the market, buying vegetables. Her phone buzzes with a message from the pharmacy about her husband's medicine. Her glasses are at home, and she cannot read the message.

Because she has turned on **Select to Speak**, she does not have to ask a stranger. She taps the message, and her phone **reads it aloud**. She learns that the medicine is ready for pickup today.

**A small safety tip.** If the phone reads private messages aloud in a crowded place, others can hear. Use earphones for bank or health messages. And never ask a stranger to read your OTP or bank messages.

**The lesson:** Bigger text and read-aloud are helpful on a good day and a bad day alike.

---

## SECTION 3 · HOW TO SET IT UP

### 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Your phone's menu names may be a little different. Use the search box in Settings if you cannot find a name.)*

**Step 1.** Open **Settings → Display**.

**Step 2.** Tap **Font size** and slide it towards the bigger letters until you can read easily.

**Step 3.** Open **Settings → Accessibility**. Find **Select to Speak** and turn it on.

**Step 4.** Now tap any words on your screen. The phone will read them aloud.

📱 **iPhone:** Use **Spoken Content** (in Settings → Accessibility).

💡 **Tip:** Bigger letters mean fewer words on the screen at a time. That is fine. Slide a little at a time until it feels comfortable.

**Scenario.** Sunita, 63, has received an important message from her bank. The words are so small that she cannot read them. She also does not want to ask anyone to read a private message.

**Question.** What can Sunita do on her own phone? *(Tick all that are correct.)*

- ☐ Make the text bigger in **Settings → Display → Font size**
- ☐ Turn on **Select to Speak** in **Settings → Accessibility**, then tap the message to hear it
- ☐ Buy a bigger phone
- ☐ Ask a stranger at the bus stop to read it aloud
- ☐ Ignore the message

**✅ Correct answer:** The first two:
- Make the text bigger in Settings → Display → Font size
- Turn on Select to Speak, then tap the message to hear it

*Why the others are wrong:* A bigger phone is expensive and not needed. A stranger should never read your bank messages.

## SECTION 8 · QUIZ

### 📝 Quiz: Read With Confidence

**5 Multiple-Choice Questions**

**MCQ 1.** Where can you make the letters on your phone bigger?
- A. The Camera app
- B. The Clock app
- C. Settings → Display → Font size
- D. The Contacts app

✅ **Answer: C.**

**MCQ 2.** Which feature lets your phone read words aloud when you tap them?
- A. Select to Speak
- B. Airplane mode
- C. Battery Saver
- D. Dark mode

✅ **Answer: A.** Turn it on in Settings → Accessibility.

**MCQ 3.** Where do you turn on Select to Speak?
- A. Settings → Battery
- B. The Photos app
- C. The Calculator app
- D. Settings → Accessibility

✅ **Answer: D.**

**MCQ 4.** What is the name of the similar feature on an iPhone?
- A. Guided Access
- B. Spoken Content
- C. Medical ID
- D. Notes

✅ **Answer: B.**

**MCQ 5.** You are in a crowded bus and want the phone to read a bank message. What is a good idea?
- A. Play it loudly so everyone can help
- B. Ask the person next to you to read it
- C. Share your OTP with a friend to check
- D. Use earphones so only you can hear it

✅ **Answer: D.** Private messages should stay private.

**3 True / False Questions**

**T/F 1.** Making the text bigger changes the words in your messages.
✅ **False.** Only the size of the letters changes.

**T/F 2.** You need to buy a new phone to get bigger letters.
✅ **False.** It is a free setting on your own phone.

**T/F 3.** It is safe to ask a stranger to read your OTP aloud for you.
✅ **False.** Never share your OTP with anyone.

**1 Scenario-Based Question**

**Scenario.** Shanta, 68, has forgotten her glasses at home. A message arrives from her son. Her phone already has Select to Speak turned on. What should she do?
- A. Tap the message and let the phone read it aloud
- B. Ask the nearest stranger to read it
- C. Delete the message
- D. Wait until she is back home

✅ **Answer: A.** That is exactly what the feature is for.
