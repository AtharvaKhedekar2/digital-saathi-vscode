# 🔒 The Safe Handover

**Card subtitle:** Let the little ones watch their rhymes, without wandering into your messages.

*Website point: "Lock your phone to one app before handing it to a child"*

---

## SECTION 1 · STORY

### 🎭 Story: "Aarav and the Rhyme Video"

👤 **Meet Anjali Deshmukh.** She is 62 and lives in Mumbai with her son's family. Her five-year-old grandson, Aarav, adores her. He has one favourite thing in the whole world: the rhyme videos on Grandma's phone.

**The situation.** Every evening, Aarav comes running: *"Aaji, phone! Rhymes!"* Anjali does not have the heart to say no.

**The problem.** Last week, while watching his rhyme, Aarav tapped a corner of the screen and ended up in her **WhatsApp**. He sent a long string of smiley faces and a blank voice note to her friend Sharda. Another day, the phone suddenly started calling the milkman. Anjali is now nervous each time she hands over the phone. She stays right beside him and keeps taking it back.

**How the phone helps.** Her daughter-in-law, Pooja, shows her **App pinning**. First, Pooja turns it on in **Settings → Security**. Then she opens the rhyme video, goes to **Recent apps**, taps the app's icon, and chooses **Pin**. *"Now Aarav can watch his rhymes, but he cannot leave this app unless someone enters your PIN, Aaji."*

**The happy ending.** Aarav presses and taps everywhere, but the screen stays on his rhymes. Anjali sits in her chair, sipping her tea, at peace. *"Now I can hand over my phone without my heart racing,"* she tells Pooja.

---

## SECTION 2 · WHAT IF?

### ❓ What if you cannot watch the child every second?

**Meet Fatima Sheikh**, 63, from Hyderabad. It is her niece's wedding, and she is in the kitchen helping with the sweets. Her cousin's eight-year-old, Zoya, wants to play a game on her phone. Fatima Sheikh cannot stand next to her all evening.

Her phone also has a **UPI payment app** and her **photos and messages**. Before handing it over, she pins the game with **App pinning**. Zoya can play the game happily. She cannot open the UPI app, her photos or her chats, even by accident, because leaving the game needs Fatima's **PIN**.

**The lesson:** App pinning protects your phone, your money and your privacy while the child enjoys one app.

**A small safety tip.** Never tell the child your PIN. And remember to unpin the app when you take the phone back.

---

## SECTION 3 · HOW TO SET IT UP

### 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Your phone's menu names may be a little different. Use the search box in Settings and type "pin" if you cannot find it.)*

**Step 1.** Open **Settings → Security**. (You can also search "pin".)

**Step 2.** Turn on **App pinning**. If it asks, choose to ask for your **PIN** before the app is unpinned.

**Step 3.** Open the app you want the child to use. Then open **Recent apps** (use the button or swipe your phone uses for this). Tap the app's **icon** at the top and choose **Pin**.

**Step 4.** Hand over the phone. The child cannot leave that app without your PIN. To take the app off pinning, enter your PIN.

📱 **iPhone:** Use **Guided Access**.

💡 **Tip:** Try it once yourself before you hand the phone to the child.

## SECTION 4 · INTERACTIVE ACTIVITY

### 🎮 Interactive Activity: "Neelam's Granddaughter"

**Scenario.** Neelam, 66, lives in Jaipur. Her granddaughter Riya, 6, wants to watch a drawing video on her phone. Neelam's phone has her WhatsApp, her photos and her UPI app.

**Question.** What should Neelam do before handing over the phone? *(Tick all that are correct.)*

- ☐ Turn on **App pinning** in **Settings → Security**
- ☐ Open the video app, go to **Recent apps**, tap its icon and choose **Pin**
- ☐ Make sure her **PIN** is needed to leave the app
- ☐ Tell Riya the phone's PIN so she can "help"
- ☐ Hand over the phone with all the apps open

**✅ Correct answer:** The first three:
- Turn on App pinning in Settings → Security
- Open the video app, go to Recent apps, tap its icon and choose Pin
- Make sure her PIN is needed to leave the app

*Why the others are wrong:* A child should never know the phone's PIN, and an open phone lets her tap into the other apps.

## SECTION 5 · REAL-LIFE BENEFITS

### 🌟 Real-Life Benefits

✅ **Your messages, photos and apps stay private** while a child uses your phone.

✅ **Your money stays safe**, because the child cannot open the payment app by accident.

✅ **No more accidental calls or messages** to friends, relatives or shops.

✅ **You can relax** and enjoy time with your grandchild, instead of watching the phone all the time.

## SECTION 8 · QUIZ

### 📝 Quiz: The Safe Handover

**5 Multiple-Choice Questions**

**MCQ 1.** What does App pinning do?
- A. Keeps the phone on one app until the PIN is entered
- B. Makes the phone louder
- C. Makes the battery last longer
- D. Deletes old apps

✅ **Answer: A.**

**MCQ 2.** Where do you usually turn on App pinning on an Android phone?
- A. The Camera app
- B. The Weather app
- C. Settings → Security
- D. The Music app

✅ **Answer: C.** You can also search "pin" in Settings.

**MCQ 3.** After the app is open, where do you go to pin it?
- A. The Calculator
- B. Recent apps
- C. The Contacts list
- D. The Voice recorder

✅ **Answer: B.** Tap the app's icon and choose **Pin**.

**MCQ 4.** What is the name of the similar feature on an iPhone?
- A. Spoken Content
- B. Medical ID
- C. Scan Documents
- D. Guided Access

✅ **Answer: D.**

**MCQ 5.** What can a child NOT do when an app is pinned?
- A. Leave the app without your PIN
- B. Watch the pinned video
- C. Pause the video
- D. Make the video louder

✅ **Answer: A.**

**3 True / False Questions**

**T/F 1.** You should tell the child your phone PIN so they can unpin the app.
✅ **False.** Never tell a child your PIN.

**T/F 2.** App pinning helps keep your photos, chats and payment apps private from a child.
✅ **True.**

**T/F 3.** After the child has finished, you should enter your PIN to unpin the app and take your phone back.
✅ **True.** Then the phone works normally again.

**1 Scenario-Based Question**

**Scenario.** At a family function, Kavita, 64, wants her nephew's six-year-old to play one game on her phone. Her phone has a payment app. What should she do first?
- A. Hand over the phone and hope for the best
- B. Give the child her PIN
- C. Pin the game with App pinning, then hand it over
- D. Switch off the internet

✅ **Answer: C.** The child can play the game, but cannot reach the payment app without her PIN.
