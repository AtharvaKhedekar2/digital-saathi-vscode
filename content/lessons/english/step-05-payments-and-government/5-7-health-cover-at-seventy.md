# 🏥 Health Cover at 70
*Everyone aged 70 and above can get the Ayushman Vay Vandana card for free hospital treatment cover.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Worry About the Hospital Bill"

### Meet Mrs. Shetty

👵 Mrs. Shetty is 71 years old and lives in Udupi. She lives with her husband on a small pension.

One night, her husband has chest pain and she rushes him to a hospital.
He is treated, and he recovers.
But the thought of the bill kept her awake: *“What if it happens again? How will we pay?”*

A neighbour tells her about the **Ayushman Vay Vandana card.**

📱 Under **Ayushman Bharat:**
- **Everyone aged 70 and above** can get the **Ayushman Vay Vandana card**
- It gives **free hospital treatment cover**
- You can apply in the **Ayushman app**, at **beneficiary.nha.gov.in**, or at a **nearby hospital or CSC**

Mrs. Shetty goes with her son to a nearby CSC and applies.

When the card arrives, she keeps it safe with her papers.
*“Now I sleep better,”* she tells her husband.

## 🤔 What If You Are Not Sure You Are Eligible?

Mr. Hegde, 72, is not sure whether he qualifies.

The rule is simple: **aged 70 and above.**
He asks at the hospital help desk, and they check for him.

💡 Always apply at the **official** places: the Ayushman app, **beneficiary.nha.gov.in**, or a nearby hospital or CSC. Never pay an agent who says you need to pay for the card.

## 🛠️ How To Apply

*(Ask a family member to help you the first time.)*

### Step 1: Check Your Age
✅ Are you **70 or above?** Then you can get the card.

### Step 2: Choose a Place to Apply
✅ The **Ayushman app**
✅ **beneficiary.nha.gov.in**
✅ A **nearby hospital** or **CSC**

### Step 3: Apply
✅ Follow the steps and apply for the **Ayushman Vay Vandana card.**

### Step 4: Keep the Card Safe
✅ Keep your card safely with your important papers.
