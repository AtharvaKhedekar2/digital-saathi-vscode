*Set up AutoPay for electricity, gas and mobile bills, and never be late again.*

## 📖 Story: "The Late Fee Again"

### Meet Mrs. Srinivasan

👵 Mrs. Srinivasan is 66 years old and lives in Coimbatore. She manages the household bills: electricity, gas and mobile.

Every month she must remember the dates.
This month, her sister's wedding keeps her busy, and the electricity bill date goes by.
A late fee is added. *“Again!”* she sighs.

Her daughter says: *“Amma, let the phone pay on time.”*

📱 With **AutoPay**:
- Your bill is paid **on time, automatically**
- You get a **message before each payment**
- You can **stop it any time**

In her UPI app she opens **Bills & recharges**, chooses her electricity bill and turns on **AutoPay.**

The next month, she gets a message before the payment, and the bill is paid on time.
No late fee.

*“I feel like I have a helper who never forgets,”* she says.

## 🤔 What If You Want to Stop It?

Mr. Menon, 69, sets up AutoPay for his mobile bill.
Later, he changes his plan and wants to stop it.

He can **stop AutoPay any time.** He gets a message before each payment, so there are no surprises.

💡 Read the message you get before each payment, so you always know what is being paid.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Menu names can differ a little in different UPI apps.)*

### Step 1: Open Your UPI App
✅ Open the UPI app you use.

### Step 2: Open Bills & Recharges
📱 Open **Bills & recharges.**

### Step 3: Choose the Bill
✅ Choose the bill, such as electricity, gas or mobile.

### Step 4: Turn On AutoPay
✅ Turn on **AutoPay.**
✅ You get a message before each payment, and you can stop it any time.
