# 👪 Trusted Hands, Safe Limits
*Let a trusted family member pay from your UPI, up to a limit you set.*

---

## 📖 Story: "When Papa Was Unwell"

### Meet Mr. Kapoor

👴 Mr. Kapoor is 75 years old and lives in Delhi. His knees hurt, and he rarely leaves the house.

When he runs out of medicines, he asks his son Varun to buy them.
Varun pays from his own account, and Mr. Kapoor pays him back later.

It works, but he feels awkward. *“Why should he pay for me?”*
And he worries: if he gave his son his own UPI details, what if there were a mistake?

Varun shows him **UPI Circle.**

📱 With **UPI Circle**:
- You **add a trusted family member**
- You set a **monthly limit**
- They can pay from **your account**, up to **that limit**
- You can **remove them any time**

Mr. Kapoor adds Varun and sets a monthly limit.

Now Varun pays for the medicines from his father's account, and nothing goes above the limit.
*“I stay in control, and he helps,”* says Mr. Kapoor.

## 🤔 What If You Want to Stop Sharing?

Mrs. Bhatia, 70, adds her niece to UPI Circle while she is recovering from surgery.
Months later, she is well again.

She **removes** her niece from UPI Circle. She can do this **any time.**

💡 Add only someone you **fully trust**, and choose a limit that feels comfortable.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Menu names can differ a little in different UPI apps.)*

### Step 1: Open UPI Circle
✅ Open **UPI Circle** in your UPI app.

### Step 2: Add a Trusted Person
✅ Add a **trusted family member.**

### Step 3: Set a Monthly Limit
✅ Choose a **monthly limit.**
✅ They can pay from your account only **up to that limit.**

### Step 4: Remove Them Any Time
✅ You can **remove them** whenever you like.
