# 🔐 Your Spare Key
*A recovery number and email mean you will never be locked out of your Google account.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Password I Forgot"

### Meet Mr. Banerjee

👴 Mr. Banerjee is 70 years old and lives in Kolkata. He is a retired schoolteacher.

His Gmail holds everything: his pension papers, his old photos, his daughter's emails from abroad.

One morning, after his phone is updated, it asks for his password.
He types what he thinks it is. Wrong.
He types another. Wrong again.

His heart sinks. *“What if I can never open my emails again?”*

His daughter calls him. *“Baba, do you remember adding a recovery phone number and email?”*
He had added them last year, when she visited and helped him.

The phone sends a code to his recovery number, and he sets a new password.
His emails are back, safe.

*“Like a spare key in the neighbour's house,”* he says.

## 🤔 What If You Lose Your Phone?

Mrs. Joshi, 66, loses her phone on a train.
She needs to sign in to her Google account on a new phone.

Because she added a **recovery phone number** and a **recovery email**, Google can confirm that it is really her and let her back in.

💡 Also **write your password down** and keep it somewhere **safe at home**. Do not keep the paper in your wallet.

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: Open Your Google Account
📱 Open: **Settings → Google → Manage your Google Account**

### Step 2: Go to Security
✅ Tap **Security**.

### Step 3: Add Recovery Details
✅ Add a **recovery phone number**.
✅ Add a **recovery email**.
✅ Use a number and an email that you can really reach, for example a family member's.

### Step 4: Write Your Password Down
✅ Write your password on paper.
✅ Keep the paper somewhere **safe at home**.

## 🎮 Interactive Activity: "Mr. Banerjee's Spare Key"

**Scenario**
Mr. Banerjee wants to make sure he is never locked out of his Google account.

**Question**
What should he do? *(Tick all that are correct.)*

☐ Add a recovery phone number
☐ Add a recovery email
☐ Write his password down and keep it safe at home
☐ Tell his password to a stranger who calls
☐ Use "1234" for every account

**Answer**
✅ Add a recovery phone number
✅ Add a recovery email
✅ Write his password down and keep it safe at home
❌ Never tell your password to anyone who calls you
❌ Simple passwords like “1234” are easy to guess
