# 🗺️ Help Is Nearby
*Find the nearest hospital, pharmacy or bus in seconds.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Fever at Night"

### Meet Mrs. Gill

👵 Mrs. Gill is 63 years old and lives in Ludhiana. Her husband and she have just moved to a new colony near their son's house.

One night, her husband has a high fever.
It is late, and they do not know this area well.
She does not know where the nearest chemist is, and she does not want to wake the neighbours.

Then she remembers that her son showed her **Google Maps.**

📱 Her phone can:
- Find the **nearest hospital or pharmacy**
- Show **how far** it is
- Show the **way to get there**

She opens Google Maps and types: **“pharmacy near me.”**
A list appears. The nearest chemist is a few minutes away, and it is open.

She gets the medicine and returns home. Her husband rests comfortably.
*“I did not have to wake anyone,”* she says.

## 🤔 What If You Need a Bus?

Mr. Das, 70, lives in Kolkata and wants to go to a friend's house in another part of the city.
He is not sure which bus to take.

He opens Google Maps, types the **place he wants to go**, and taps the **bus icon.**
He sees **bus routes and timings**, where they are available.

💡 If a route or timing is not shown for your area, ask at the bus stand or a trusted person.

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: Open Google Maps
✅ Tap the **Google Maps** app.

### Step 2: Search for Help
📱 In the search bar type: **“hospital near me”** or **“pharmacy near me”**

### Step 3: Choose a Place
✅ Tap a place in the list to see how far it is and how to reach it.

### Step 4: For Buses
✅ Type where you want to go.
✅ Tap the **bus icon** to see routes and timings, where available.
