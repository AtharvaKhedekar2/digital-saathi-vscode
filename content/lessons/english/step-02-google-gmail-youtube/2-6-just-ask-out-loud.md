# 🗣️ Just Ask Out Loud
*Ask Google anything by voice, in Hindi, Marathi or English.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Question I Could Not Type"

### Meet Mr. Kamble

👴 Mr. Kamble is 72 years old and lives in Kolhapur. He worked in a sugar factory, and he speaks only Marathi.

His grandson asked him: *“Ajoba, what is the weather going to be tomorrow? Shall we plan the picnic?”*

Mr. Kamble wants to find out. But typing on the phone is slow for him, and the keyboard is mostly in English.
His fingers are stiff, and he makes many mistakes. He gives up.

Then his granddaughter shows him the **microphone** in the Google app.

📱 His phone can:
- **Listen** when he speaks
- Understand **Hindi, Marathi or English**
- Show the answer, in **his language** when it is set that way

He taps the microphone and says, in Marathi, what he wants to know about tomorrow's weather.
The answer appears.

*“I just spoke, and the phone understood!”* he says with wide eyes.

## 🤔 What If Your Hands Are Busy or Your Fingers Hurt?

Mrs. Pawar, 69, has pain in her fingers. Today she is cooking.
She wants to know how long to boil an egg.

She taps the microphone and asks in Hindi.
She gets the answer without touching the keyboard.

💡 To get the results in your own language, choose it in the **Google app's Settings.** A family member can help you do it once.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: Open the Google App
✅ Open the **Google** app.

### Step 2: Tap the Microphone
📱 Tap the **microphone** in the search bar.

### Step 3: Speak
✅ Speak clearly in **Hindi, Marathi or English.**
✅ Ask your question in a quiet place.

### Step 4: Choose Your Language for Results
📱 Open the Google app's **Settings**.
✅ Choose your language to get results in it.
