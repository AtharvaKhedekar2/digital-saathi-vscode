# 🎮 Say No to Strangers
*Children: never share your name, school, photos or address with people you meet in games or chats.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Friendly Player"

### Meet Rohit

🧒 Rohit is 11 years old and lives in Indore. He loves his online cricket game, and he plays after homework.

One day, a player he does not know sends him a message in the game chat:
*“Hi, you play so well! What is your name? Which school do you go to? Send me a photo?”*

Rohit feels special. Nobody has ever praised his playing like this.
He is about to type his school's name.

Then he remembers what his teacher said: **“Never tell people you meet in games or chats your name, school, photos or address.”**

📱 Rohit remembers:
- A stranger online may **not be who they say**
- **Never** share your **name, school, photos or address**
- **Tell a parent** if someone asks

He does not answer. He goes straight to his mother and shows her.

His mother says: *“You did the right thing. I am proud of you.”*

---

## 🤔 What If the Stranger Says “Do Not Tell Your Parents”?

Priya, 12, gets a message: *“This is our secret. Do not tell your parents.”*

A friendly person never needs a secret from your parents.
That is a big warning sign.

She shows the message to her father at once.

💡 **Children:** if someone makes you uncomfortable, tell a parent or a teacher. You will never be in trouble for telling.

---

## 🛠️ For Children and Parents

### Children — Step 1: Stay Private
✅ **Never** tell people you meet in games or chats your name, school, photos or address.

### Children — Step 2: Tell a Parent
✅ If someone asks for these things, **tell a parent.**

### Parents — Step 3: Friends Only
✅ Set the game or app chat to **friends only.**

### Parents — Step 4: Talk Often
✅ Talk to your child about who they meet online.
✅ Make sure they know they can always tell you anything.
