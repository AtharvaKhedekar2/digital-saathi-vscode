# 🚨 The Call That Wasn't Police
*Real police never arrest anyone on a video call. Hang up and call 1930.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Officer on the Screen"

### Meet Mr. Ghosh

👴 Mr. Ghosh is 70 years old and lives in Kolkata. He is a retired bank manager, and he has never had a problem with the law.

One afternoon, an unknown number calls him on video.
A man in a police uniform sits in front of a desk. *“Mr. Ghosh, a parcel in your name has illegal items. You are under digital arrest. Do not hang up. Do not tell anyone. Transfer money to settle the case.”*

Mr. Ghosh's hands go cold. His heart pounds. He is frightened.

But a line from the Digital Saathi class comes back to him: **“Police never arrest anyone on a video call, and never ask for money to ‘settle a case.’”**

📱 What he remembers:
- Real police, the CBI or courts **never arrest anyone on a video call**
- They **never ask for money** to settle a case
- A frightening call in uniform is a **scam**

He takes a breath and **hangs up.**
Then he calls **1930**, the cyber crime helpline, and tells them.

His family hears about it that evening, and they all feel relieved. *“You did the right thing, Baba.”*

---

## 🤔 What If the Caller Says “Do Not Tell Anyone”?

Mrs. Iyer, 65, gets a similar call. The caller says: *“Do not tell your family or you will be in trouble.”*

That is a big warning sign. Scammers want you **alone and scared**, so you do not think clearly.

She hangs up and tells her son at once.

💡 If anyone in uniform threatens you on a call, **hang up** and call **1930** or your **local police.**

---

## 🛠️ What To Do — Step by Step

### Step 1: Stay Calm
✅ Take a deep breath. A real arrest does not happen over a video call.

### Step 2: Do Not Pay Anything
✅ Never send money to “settle a case.”
✅ Never share your Aadhaar, bank details or OTP.

### Step 3: Hang Up
✅ Cut the call. You do not have to be polite.

### Step 4: Call for Help
📱 Call **1930** or your **local police.**
✅ Tell a family member at once.
