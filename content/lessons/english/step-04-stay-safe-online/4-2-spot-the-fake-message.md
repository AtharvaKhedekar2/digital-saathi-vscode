# 🚩 Spot the Fake Message
*KYC, electricity and parcel messages with a link or a number are fake. Check only in the official app.*

---

## 📖 Story: "Your Electricity Will Be Cut Tonight"

### Meet Mrs. Pandey

👵 Mrs. Pandey is 62 years old and lives in Lucknow. She lives with her husband and pays all the household bills on time.

One evening, her phone buzzes with a message:
*“Your electricity will be cut tonight! Call this number now.”*

Her husband is resting, and the fan is running. Her heart skips.
She is about to call the number.

Then she remembers a lesson: **messages that rush you, with a link or a number, are fake.**

📱 She remembers:
- **“Your KYC is expiring”**, **“your electricity will be cut tonight”** or **“your parcel is stuck”** are common fake messages
- A **link** or a **number** in them is the trap
- The only safe place to check is the **official app** or the **office**

She does not call. She opens the **official electricity app.**
It shows: *No dues. Next bill on the 15th.*

*“It was a trick!”* she says. She deletes the message.

---

## 🤔 What If the Message Has Your Name and Looks Real?

Mr. Verma, 68, gets a message: *“Your parcel is stuck. Update your address here.”* It even has his name.

Scammers can find names easily. A real-looking message can still be fake.

He does not tap the link. He checks with the courier's **official app.**

💡 Do not tap links or call numbers in such messages. Check yourself, in the official app or at the office.

---

## 🛠️ What To Do — Step by Step

### Step 1: Pause
✅ Do not rush. Fear and hurry are the scammer's tools.

### Step 2: Do Not Tap or Call
✅ Do not tap the link.
✅ Do not call the number in the message.

### Step 3: Check Yourself
📱 Open the **official app**, or visit the **office**, and check.

### Step 4: Delete or Report
✅ Delete the message.
✅ Tell your family so they are warned too.
