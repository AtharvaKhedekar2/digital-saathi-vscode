# ✍️ Fill It In, Press Submit
*A Google Form is just questions on a screen. Answer, then press Submit.*

---

## 📖 Story: “The Quiz With No Pass or Fail”

### Meet Mrs. Pawar

👵 Mrs. Pawar is 58 years old and lives in Nashik. She is a housewife who loves cooking.

Her Saathi sends her a link on WhatsApp: “Please fill the Pre-Assessment. It has no pass or fail.”

Mrs. Pawar is nervous. “What if I make a mistake?” she asks.

Her Saathi smiles. “This is only to help us understand where to begin.”

📱 She taps the link and her phone:
- Opens the form in the browser
- Lets her tap or type her answers
- Sends her answers when she taps **Submit**

She reads each question slowly. When she does not know an answer, she chooses **I am not sure**.
At the bottom she taps **Submit**, and a message says her answer has been recorded.
*“So simple,”* she says.

---

## 🤔 What If You Close the Page Too Early?

Mrs. Pawar once closes a form before tapping Submit. Her answers are not sent.

She simply opens the link again and starts over. It takes only a few minutes.

💡 Only the **Submit** button sends your answers.

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

### Step 1: Tap the link
✅ Tap the form’s link. It opens in your browser.
✅ Some forms ask you to sign in with Gmail first.

### Step 2: Read each question slowly
✅ Tap an answer, or type your own.
✅ If you do not know, choose **I am not sure**.

### Step 3: Scroll to the bottom
✅ Check that you have answered every question.

### Step 4: Tap Submit
✅ Tap **Submit**.
✅ Look for the message that your answer has been recorded.
