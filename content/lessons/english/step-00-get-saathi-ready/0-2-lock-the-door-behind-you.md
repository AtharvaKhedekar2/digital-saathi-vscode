# 🚪 Lock the Door Behind You
*Sign in safely, and always sign out on a shared computer.*

---

## 📖 Story: “The Open Inbox”

### Meet Mr. Chopra

👴 Mr. Chopra is 70 years old and lives in Ludhiana. He checks his email at a nearby cyber café.

One morning he reads his email, gets a phone call, and rushes out of the café.

He forgets to sign out. The next customer sits down at the same computer and sees Mr. Chopra’s inbox.

A kind young man at the café notices and calls him back just in time.

📱 The young man shows him what to remember:
- Sign in only on **gmail.com** or the **Gmail app**
- Type any code Google sends on the screen only
- On a shared computer, **sign out** when you finish

The next day, Mr. Chopra finishes reading his email. He taps his round picture at the top right and taps **Sign out**.
*“Like locking the door behind me,”* he says.

---

## 🤔 What If a Message Asks for Your Password?

Mr. Chopra gets a message: “Your account will be closed today. Send your password and OTP to save it.”

He remembers that Google never asks for a password or OTP by call or message. He ignores it.

💡 When in doubt, do not reply. Open Gmail yourself and look for a real warning there.

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

### Step 1: Go to the real page
📱 Open the **Gmail app**, or type **gmail.com** yourself.
✅ Do not sign in from a link in a message.

### Step 2: Sign in
✅ Type your Gmail address and tap **Next**.
✅ Type your password and tap **Next**.

### Step 3: Keep codes to yourself
✅ If Google sends a code, type it on the screen only.
✅ Never read it out on a call.

### Step 4: Sign out on a shared computer
📱 Tap your **round picture** at the top right, then tap **Sign out**.
✅ Never save your password on a shared or public computer.
