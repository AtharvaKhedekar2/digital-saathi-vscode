# 📵 The Unknown Caller Shield
*Stop calls from strangers ringing on WhatsApp, and still see who called.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Afternoon Nap"

### Meet Mr. Chauhan

👴 Mr. Chauhan is 69 years old and lives in Jaipur. After lunch, he likes a short afternoon nap.

But lately, strange numbers keep calling him on WhatsApp.
Sometimes the phone rings three times in an hour.
He does not know who is calling, and he is a little scared.

Once he picked up, and a stranger started talking about a “prize.” He cut the call, but he felt uneasy for hours.

His nephew Ishaan shows him a setting called **Silence unknown callers.**

📱 His phone can:
- **Silence** WhatsApp calls from numbers **not in his contacts**
- Still show those calls in his **call list**
- Ring normally for **family and friends**

He opens **WhatsApp Settings → Privacy → Calls** and turns on **Silence unknown callers.**

The next afternoon, he naps in peace.
When he wakes, he sees two missed calls from unknown numbers in his call list. He ignores them.

*“My phone has a guard at the door,”* he says.

## 🤔 What If a Real Person, Like a Delivery Boy, Calls?

Mrs. Anand, 65, is waiting for a delivery.
The delivery boy's number is not in her contacts, so her phone does not ring.

Later, she sees the call in her **call list**, because silenced calls **still show there.**
She calls him back, and the parcel arrives.

💡 Check your call list once in a while, so you do not miss anything important.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(WhatsApp on Android and iPhone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: Open WhatsApp Settings
✅ Open **WhatsApp** and go to **Settings**.

### Step 2: Open Privacy
📱 Tap **Privacy**.

### Step 3: Open Calls
📱 Tap **Calls**.

### Step 4: Silence Unknown Callers
✅ Turn on **Silence unknown callers.**
✅ Calls from numbers not in your contacts will not ring. They still show in your call list.
