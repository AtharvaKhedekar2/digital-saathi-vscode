# 📍 Walk Home Together
*Let your family follow you on the map until you reach home.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Evening After the Temple"

### Meet Mrs. Rao

👵 Mrs. Rao is 64 years old and lives in Bengaluru. Every Thursday she walks to the temple in the evening.

The walk home is short, but in winter it gets dark early.
Her daughter Kavya, who works late, worries every Thursday.
*“Amma, call me when you reach home,”* she says. But Mrs. Rao often forgets.

Last week, Kavya called six times while her mother was in the temple, and she was very anxious.

Then Kavya shows her mother **live location** in WhatsApp.

📱 Her phone can:
- Share where she is, **live**, on a map
- Let her family **follow her** until she reaches home
- Stop on its own after the time she chooses

Mrs. Rao opens her chat with Kavya, taps **📎 → Location → Share live location**, and chooses **1 hour.**
Kavya watches the small dot move along the map, from the temple to the gate.

*“Reached,”* Kavya smiles, without making a single call.

## 🤔 What If You Are Taking an Auto or Taxi Alone?

Mr. Sharma, 68, takes an auto home from the railway station at night.
His son is worried.

Before leaving, he shares his **live location** with his son for the length of the ride.
His son can see him moving along the road, and both feel at ease.

💡 Share your live location only with family and people you trust. You choose how long to share.

---

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(WhatsApp on Android and iPhone. Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: Open a WhatsApp Chat
✅ Open the chat of a family member you trust.

### Step 2: Tap the Attach Icon
📱 Tap **📎**.

### Step 3: Share Live Location
📱 Tap **Location → Share live location**.

### Step 4: Choose How Long
✅ Choose how long to share, for example **1 hour**.
✅ Your family can follow you on the map until you reach home.

## 🎮 Interactive Activity: "Mrs. Rao's Thursday Walk"

**Scenario**
Mrs. Rao is walking home from the temple in the evening. Her daughter wants to know she is safe.

**Question**
What should she do? *(Tick all that are correct.)*

☐ Open the chat with her daughter
☐ Tap 📎 → Location → Share live location
☐ Choose how long to share, for example 1 hour
☐ Share her live location in a large public group
☐ Turn off the phone

**Answer**
✅ Open the chat with her daughter
✅ Tap 📎 → Location → Share live location
✅ Choose how long to share, for example 1 hour
❌ A large public group should never see where you are
❌ A switched-off phone cannot share anything

🏆 **Safe Traveller Badge Earned!**
