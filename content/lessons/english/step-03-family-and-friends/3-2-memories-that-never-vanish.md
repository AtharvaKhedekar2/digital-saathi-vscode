# 💾 Memories That Never Vanish
*Back up your WhatsApp chats and photos, so a lost phone does not mean lost memories.*

---

## 📖 Story: "The Phone Left on the Train"

### Meet Mr. Mehta

👴 Mr. Mehta is 68 years old and lives in Surat. His phone holds years of family photos: his late wife, his grandchildren, the pilgrimage they made together.

One day on a train, his phone slips out of his pocket and is gone.

He is heartbroken. It is not the phone he mourns. It is the photos and the family chats.

*“Everything is gone,”* he tells his son.

His son says: *“Papa, I have turned on your backup. Many of your photos and chats are safe.”*
Months ago, he had set it up on Mr. Mehta's phone.

📱 The backup:
- Saves his **WhatsApp chats** every day to **Google Drive**
- Saves his **photos** in **Google Photos**
- Keeps them safe, **even if the phone is lost**

On his new phone, Mr. Mehta signs in, and his photos and chats come back.

*“Thank you for keeping my memories safe,”* he says, with tears of joy.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. On iPhone, WhatsApp backs up to iCloud. Menu names can differ a little on your phone.)*

### Step 1: Back Up WhatsApp
📱 Open WhatsApp: **Settings → Chats → Chat backup**

### Step 2: Turn On Daily Backup
✅ Choose a **daily** backup to **Google Drive**.
✅ **iPhone:** choose **iCloud**.

### Step 3: Back Up Your Photos
📱 Open the **Google Photos** app.
✅ Turn on **backup**.

### Step 4: Check Now and Then
✅ Ask a family member to look at your backup once in a while.
