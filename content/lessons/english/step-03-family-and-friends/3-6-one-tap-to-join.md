# 🔗 One Tap to Join
*Join a class or family meeting with just one link.*

---

## 📖 Story: "Didi's Birthday on Zoom"

### Meet Mr. Naik

👴 Mr. Naik is 71 years old and lives in Panaji. His younger sister, Didi, lives in Canada, and her birthday is on Sunday.

The family has planned a video meeting with everyone, in different cities and countries.
His niece sends a **link** on WhatsApp: *“Uncle, tap this at 6 pm.”*

Mr. Naik is nervous. *“What if I press the wrong thing? What if I cannot find the meeting?”*
He has never joined a meeting before.

His grandson Neil sits with him and shows him how simple it is.

📱 His phone can:
- Open the meeting with **one tap on the link**
- Show his **own name** so others know it is him
- Let him **join** and see his whole family

At 6 pm, he taps the link, allows the **camera and microphone**, checks his name and taps **Join.**

The screen fills with faces: Didi, cousins, nieces, all singing.
*“I am at the party, from my own sofa,”* he says, delighted.

## 🤔 What If It Is a Class and Not a Party?

Mrs. Kamat, 66, joins a free online learning class with a link from the teacher.
At class time, she taps the **Google Meet** link.

She allows the camera and microphone and taps **Join.**

💡 Join only links sent by people you know. Do not tap meeting links from strangers.

## 🛠️ How To Set It Up

*(Android phone. Google Meet and Zoom work in a similar way. Menu names can differ a little.)*

### Step 1: Receive the Link
✅ Someone sends a **Google Meet** or **Zoom** link.

### Step 2: Tap the Link at the Meeting Time
✅ Tap the link when the meeting is about to start.

### Step 3: Allow Camera and Microphone
✅ Tap **Allow** for the camera and the microphone, so others can see and hear you.

### Step 4: Check Your Name and Join
✅ Check that your **name** is right.
✅ Tap **Join.**
