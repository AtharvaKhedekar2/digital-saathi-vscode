# Digital Saathi Google Classrooms: full content

This file lists **everything** the script `classroom-setup.gs` puts into the six classrooms: the class details, the topics in order and every post. Use it to review the content, or to build a class by hand if you ever can't run the script. Lesson titles and *How to do it* texts are the same as the website's Learn section.

Video and PDF suggestions are ideas for the team to record or prepare; no outside links are included. The only links are the team's own Pre- and Post-Assessment Google Forms.

## Step 01 · Digital Saathi – Smartphone Basics

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Class name | Digital Saathi – Smartphone Basics |
| Section | Step 01 of 6 · Digital literacy |
| Room | Online · learn at your own pace |
| Description heading | Welcome to Step 01: Smartphone Basics |
| Website step | Step 01 · Smartphone Basics |

**Description**

> By the end of this class you will use the most helpful settings on your own smartphone with confidence: emergency SOS, bigger text and read-aloud, a document scanner, a child lock, voice reminders and a battery that lasts all day.  
>
> What you will learn:  
> • Send an SOS in one press and show emergency contacts on your lock screen  
> • Make text bigger and let your phone read it aloud  
> • Turn your camera into a document scanner  
> • Lock your phone to one app before handing it to a child  
> • Set medicine and bill reminders just by speaking  
> • Make your battery last the whole day  
>
> How this class works:  
> 1. Start with the “Start here” topic.  
> 2. Go through Lessons 1, 2 and 3 in order. Each lesson post shows how to do it, a “Try it now” task, and a suggested video and handout.  
> 3. Do the practical activities on your own phone.  
> 4. When you have finished all six Digital Saathi classes, check your skills with the Post-Assessment.  
>
> Subject: Digital literacy · Step 01 of 6 in the Digital Saathi learning path (the same as Step 01 in the Learn section of the Digital Saathi website).  
>
> Safety first: never post an OTP, PIN, password, Aadhaar number or bank details in this class — not even to your teacher.

### Topic: Lesson 1 · Safety and easy reading


**1.1 · 🆘 Send an SOS in one press and show emergency contacts on your lock screen**: *Material*

> How to do it:  
> On Android open Settings → Safety & emergency (the name varies by phone) → Emergency SOS. Pressing the power button 5 times quickly then calls for help. Add emergency contacts and medical information there so they show on your lock screen. On iPhone, use the Health app → Medical ID.  
>
> Try it now:  
> Open the Emergency SOS setting, add one emergency contact, then lock your phone and check that the contact shows on the lock screen.  
>
> Suggested video: A 2-minute screen recording of setting up Emergency SOS and emergency contacts on an Android phone. (Suggestion for the teacher: record it yourself, or attach a video you have checked from an official source.)  
>
> Suggested PDF or handout: A wallet card: “Press the power button 5 times for SOS”, with the emergency numbers 112 and 1930.  
>
> हिंदी: एक बटन से SOS भेजें और लॉक स्क्रीन पर इमरजेंसी कॉन्टैक्ट दिखाएं  
> मराठी: एका बटणाने SOS पाठवा आणि लॉक स्क्रीनवर आपत्कालीन संपर्क दाखवा  


**1.2 · 🔠 Make text bigger and let your phone read it aloud**: *Material*

> How to do it:  
> Settings → Display → Font size makes the letters bigger. Turn on Select to Speak in Settings → Accessibility, then tap any text to hear it read aloud. (iPhone: Spoken Content.)  
>
> Try it now:  
> Make the font one size bigger, then use Select to Speak to hear this lesson read aloud.  
>
> Suggested video: A screen recording of changing the font size and turning on Select to Speak. (Suggestion for the teacher: record it yourself, or attach a video you have checked from an official source.)  
>
> Suggested PDF or handout: A picture guide of Settings → Display → Font size, with an arrow on each screen.  
>
> हिंदी: अक्षर बड़े करें और फ़ोन से पढ़कर सुनें  
> मराठी: अक्षरे मोठी करा आणि फोनकडून वाचून ऐका  


### Topic: Lesson 2 · Camera scanner and child lock


**2.3 · 📷 Turn your camera into a document scanner**: *Material*

> How to do it:  
> In the Google Drive app tap + → Scan and point the camera at the paper. It becomes a clear PDF you can send on WhatsApp. On iPhone, use Notes → Scan Documents.  
>
> Try it now:  
> Scan any paper at home (a bill or a recipe) and send the PDF to a family member on WhatsApp.  
>
> Suggested video: A screen recording of scanning a paper with Google Drive and sharing the PDF. (Suggestion for the teacher: record it yourself, or attach a video you have checked from an official source.)  
>
> Suggested PDF or handout: A picture guide of the four taps to scan with Google Drive.  
>
> हिंदी: अपने कैमरे को डॉक्यूमेंट स्कैनर बनाएं  
> मराठी: तुमच्या कॅमेऱ्याला डॉक्युमेंट स्कॅनर बनवा  


**2.4 · 📌 Lock your phone to one app before handing it to a child**: *Material*

> How to do it:  
> Turn on App pinning in Settings → Security (search for “pin”). Open the app, go to Recent apps, tap its icon and choose Pin. The child cannot leave that app without your PIN. (iPhone: Guided Access.)  
>
> Try it now:  
> Turn on App pinning, pin one app, and check that the phone asks for your PIN when you try to leave it.  
>
> Suggested video: A screen recording of turning on App pinning and pinning an app. (Suggestion for the teacher: record it yourself, or attach a video you have checked from an official source.)  
>
> Suggested PDF or handout: A parents' checklist: before you hand your phone to a child.  
>
> हिंदी: बच्चे को फ़ोन देने से पहले उसे एक ही ऐप में लॉक करें  
> मराठी: मुलाच्या हातात फोन देण्याआधी तो एकाच ॲपमध्ये लॉक करा  


### Topic: Lesson 3 · Reminders and battery


**3.5 · 💊 Set medicine and bill reminders just by speaking**: *Material*

> How to do it:  
> Say “Hey Google, remind me to take my medicine every day at 9 am” or “Remind me to pay the electricity bill on the 5th”. Your phone will remind you on time.  
>
> Try it now:  
> Say “Hey Google, remind me to drink water at 4 pm today” and check that the reminder arrives.  
>
> Suggested video: A short clip of setting a medicine reminder by voice. (Suggestion for the teacher: record it yourself, or attach a video you have checked from an official source.)  
>
> Suggested PDF or handout: A list of ready-to-say reminder sentences for medicines and bills.  
>
> हिंदी: सिर्फ़ बोलकर दवा और बिल के रिमाइंडर लगाएं  
> मराठी: फक्त बोलून औषध आणि बिलांचे रिमाइंडर लावा  


**3.6 · 🔋 Make your battery last the whole day**: *Material*

> How to do it:  
> Turn on Battery Saver in Settings → Battery, keep the screen brightness low and close apps you are not using. Switch off Location and Bluetooth when you don't need them.  
>
> Try it now:  
> Turn on Battery Saver and see how many hours the battery is now expected to last.  
>
> Suggested video: A screen recording of Battery Saver and of switching off Location and Bluetooth. (Suggestion for the teacher: record it yourself, or attach a video you have checked from an official source.)  
>
> Suggested PDF or handout: Battery tips on one page.  
>
> हिंदी: बैटरी पूरे दिन चलाएं  
> मराठी: बॅटरी दिवसभर टिकवा

> Try it now:

### Topic: Lesson 2 · Safe YouTube and a safe Google account


**2.3 · 🧒 Make YouTube safe for children**: *Material*

> How to do it:  
> For young children, use the YouTube Kids app. On regular YouTube, turn on Restricted Mode in Settings → General to hide videos meant for adults.  
>
> Try it now:  
> Turn on Restricted Mode in YouTube, or install YouTube Kids for a child at home.  
>
> Suggested video: A screen recording of turning on Restricted Mode in YouTube. (Suggestion for the teacher: record it yourself, or attach a video you have checked from an official source.)  
>
> Suggested PDF or handout: A parents' checklist for safe YouTube.  
>
> हिंदी: YouTube को बच्चों के लिए सुरक्षित बनाएं  
> मराठी: YouTube मुलांसाठी सुरक्षित करा  


**2.4 · 🔐 Never get locked out of your Google account**: *Material*

> How to do it:  
> In Settings → Google → Manage your Google Account → Security, add a recovery phone number and a recovery email. Write your password down and keep it somewhere safe at home.  
>
> Try it now:  
> Check that your recovery phone number and recovery email are correct.  
>
> Suggested video: A screen recording of adding a recovery phone and email in Manage your Google Account. (Suggestion for the teacher: record it yourself, or attach a video you have checked from an official source.)

> Suggested PDF or handout: A fill-in card for your Google account details, to keep safe at home (never post it here).
